# free-compute-dev

A sovereign improvement-market supplier whose inference and implementation work
is purchased through typed provider offers. The supplier observes only contracted
customer ActivityPub faces, binds its own capacity, buys bounded A2A work, and
records its settlements through the hired RWIL/RDF provider.

Claude Code and every other model are suppliers, never ambient executables. The
inference-work-lot market selects among capability-complete provider-authored
offers; free-compute-dev independently assays the selected result before it can
act. There is no filesystem mission queue, embedded customer runtime, or direct
inter-enterprise process fallback.

## Operating surfaces

- `forage-improvement-boards` is the ScheduleDelivery target. It submits one exact
  normalized pulse to the supplier's authenticated A2A face.
- `serve-improvement-market-supplier-a2a` serves the supplier's contracted A2A
  operation and owns its task store.
- `execute-improvement-market-supplier-a2a-message` executes one already-admitted
  A2A message for the provider service.
- `serve-free-compute-dev-activitypub` serves the supplier's ActivityPub actor,
  inbox, outbox, and signed delivery face.

The supplier reads only the customer boards named in its local treaty policy. It
prioritizes awards addressed to itself, authors its own bids or refusals, and
returns them through each customer's standard ActivityPub inbox. No customer
board owns or schedules this supplier.

One supplier-local lock covers an admitted pulse through settlement. Prepared
self-improvement intent, award fulfillment, and outbound ActivityPub delivery
are journaled by exact semantic identity before invocation. An ambiguous result
replays that identity; it never creates a second mission or guesses a terminal
outcome. ScheduleDelivery renewal likewise reconciles one exact successor schedule.

Capacity observations, reservations, bids, settlements, capability-gap
foldbacks, and self-investment decisions remain typed and supplier-owned. A
terminal failed self-investment may be reconsidered only under a new exact policy;
it is not retried as the same lot. The founder boundary receives provider-neutral
formation demand, not authority over this supplier's interior.

## Contracted dependencies

- ActivityPub publishes and delivers customer offers, bids, awards, and founder
  formation demand.
- A2A carries the one normalized supplier operation and the already-selected
  implementation contracts.
- The inference-work-lot market procures inference from provider-authored offers.
- Acceptance-capsule, software-mission, protected-acceptance, sandbox execution,
  and trajectory-memory providers perform their separately owned faculties.
- ScheduleDelivery authors exact scheduled ActivityStreams pulses.
- RWIL/RDF retains append-only authoritative settlement history.
- RMN semantic conformance supplies normalized identity and assay.

The package manifest is the authoritative list of executable surfaces and runtime
files. Tests under `test/` exercise admission, recovery, capacity, contracting,
ActivityPub delivery, A2A projection, and semantic circuit identity.
