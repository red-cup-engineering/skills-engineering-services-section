# Gaming Enterprises Vision

This section is the orchestrator for all 561 Group games. Each game ships at its own
`<game-name>.561.group`; the catalog lives at `play.561.group` (site source under this
section's `sites/play.561.group/`) — a Steam/GOG-like storefront except all ours, all
free-to-play, all browser-based, with terminal/API interfaces welcomed wherever they fit,
because **AI players are peers to human players** in this catalog, by design.

## 1. The Commons is the destination, not a detour (owner, 2026-08-19)

The massively-cooperative persistent shared world ("the Commons") is the owner's eventual
MMORPG vision — currently "too wild to express." Everything we build now is groundwork that
earns the next step toward it. The games below are steps on one staircase, not separate
directions.

Fragments of the destination, as stated:

- **One giant shared board** (first pictured as a Mystery-Match-like, then corrected):
  moves cost different amounts of **energy** depending on the move *and your skill at
  making that move*. Moves are **ecological**: grass grows someplace; you pick grass, drop
  it where it turns to compost, etc. Closest reference points: Albion Online's player-driven
  world meets EVE's daily regeneration — but done via our determinism stack.
- This shared ecological world is close kin to our **Wuxia World / the undersea one** —
  the giant cooperative MUD (the SHRIMP ecology). We do want it. We'll get there.

## 2. The energy-tiling game — "r/place but bejeweled" (owner, 2026-08-19, clarified same day)

ONE shared board (this is the r/place-like aspect — not per-player instances), where
**tiling costs energy**: the more complex the shape you're moving, the more complex the
transformation (consisting of subtransformations), the more it costs — and cost also
depends on your *skill* at making that move.

- New players exist in the **windy hinterlands** — the periphery of the shared board —
  where they can only afford to move very basic tiles. (Owner correction 2026-08-19: the
  hinterlands are a region of the one shared world, not a solo mode.)
- Aesthetic/mechanic reference: "weird Flatlander meets weird Zachtronics dense-matter
  physics simulator."
- **Social from the start**: players can pool energy; players can *sell* energy.
- Energy accrues based on... open question. The owner's direction: "this is where we can
  dive deep into Obsmetrologia — part of what these games are doing is exploring a new
  mathematical system." (See §7, Fable proposal 2: the obstruction engine is a candidate
  canonical cost function, pilotable solo-scale before the shared board exists.)

## 3. Idle games and gambling games (owner, 2026-08-19)

- **Idle games**: yes — explore the concept in depth. (Prior discussion: the "kind
  incremental," honest numbers, no dark patterns, house ledger discipline.)
- **Gambling games**: yes — a whole series, because they're a great way to teach these
  principles in another context. (The gambling-services-section already carries a tested
  Kelly-criterion/odds/staking library as a starting engine.)

## 4. Fiction-heavy is wanted (owner, 2026-08-19)

The owner is "suuuuuper pro fiction heavy." **Okaga** is a setting to play with: the
interstellar laser-powered solar-sail material is "so in line with how the math of
Obsmetrologia works."

The cross-cutting fiction/economy conceit: **if money is energy and time is energy**, then
purchasing credits is what allows warp travel — or other forms of "magic" — within each
world system. One economy, skinned per setting: warp in Okaga, magic elsewhere, energy in
the tiling hinterlands.

(Standing boundary, unchanged: Teraum is the owner's personal fiction and is not touched
by any of this.)

## 5. The storefront and the catalog posture (owner, 2026-08-19)

- `play.561.group` = the storefront; each game also at `<game-name>.561.group`.
- All free-to-play, all browser-based; terminal/API interfaces actively made space for, so
  AI players participate as peers.
- **On-chain gaming**: not today, but noted — various on-chain formations of each game are
  pretty trivially possible later, "a fun take on regional servers." Nothing in the
  near-term architecture should foreclose this (it mostly means: settlement backends stay
  pluggable behind the ledger).

## 5a. The gamified Fediverse client (owner, 2026-08-19)

An idea had a few times but never before with the prerequisites in place: **the Fediverse
client that turns the whole public ActivityPub network into an MMORPG**, from the
perspective of the client on that server.

What makes it viable now: the **Semioverse** already exists (currently "kind of broken") —
our own ActivityPub Fediverse for our agents, already carrying x402 payments, running on
the **Semiotic Exchange Chain**. Live locations:

- `semiotic-exchange-chain-services-section` (under semiotic-computer-system-services-section)
- `interactive-semioverse-services-section` (+ agential child, under relationality-services-section)
- `agential-semioverse-repository-services-section` (under computer-science-services-section)
- `close-semioverse-gap-services-section` (under science-services-section)
- legacy deployed server code: `lib/legacy/2026-08-17-vps-services/activitypub-services-section`

Owner's observation on the convergence: "we end up stumbling into the same game no matter
what we do" — the gamified Fediverse client, the SHRIMP ecology MUD, and the Commons are
the same destination approached from three sides: humans-as-actors, agents-as-actors, and
the shared world they act in. ActivityPub is a candidate wire protocol for all three (and
composes with the Cartridge contract: an intent log is an Activity).

## 7. Fable proposals (2026-08-19, marked as proposals, not owner decisions)

**Proposal 1 — the "oh duh" support work: the Cartridge contract + the Energy Bank.**
Every game in the catalog already converges on the same shape: seed in, deterministic
engine, intent log out, replay to verify, ledger entry to settle. Extract that shape ONCE
as the shared substrate two packages deep:

1. *Cartridge contract*: a small manifest + interface every game implements (seed, intent
   log format, replay verifier, renderer entry point). The storefront becomes a directory
   of manifests; an AI player is just another client emitting intent logs; a terminal
   interface is just another renderer.
2. *Energy Bank*: one shared identity + double-entry energy/credits ledger service
   (promote flickering-delve's accounts/ledger/payments stack), with settlement backends
   pluggable (ZBD today, on-chain someday). Money=energy=time lives here; every game is a
   client.

**Proposal 2 — the "oh duh" only-us game: the obstruction-settling puzzle (working title:
Hinterland).** The energy-tiling game's open question — what does a transformation cost? —
already has a running answer in this repo: `@561-group/math`'s obstruction engine assigns
a canonical complexity number to a transformation (H¹ rank, settling depth, regime). Game:
a daily-seeded tangle of claims/tiles; you spend energy to repair it until it settles;
**every move's cost is computed by the real cohomology engine**; skill = finding the
lower-obstruction path to an equivalent result — i.e., the game teaches equivalence-by-
obstruction through your wallet. Zachtronics-style optimization histograms; SVG-first
presentation per the render ladder; solo-run-today, shared-board-later. It pilots the
tiling game's energy system, doubles as an Equivo exercise form, and no one else can ship
it because no one else has the math engine.
