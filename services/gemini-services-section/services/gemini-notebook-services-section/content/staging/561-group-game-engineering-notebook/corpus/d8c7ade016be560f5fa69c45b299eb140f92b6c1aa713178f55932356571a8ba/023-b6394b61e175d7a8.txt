# Consolidated Fabrication Protocol: Base Substrate, Read/Write Channels, and Open Steps

**Status: draft.** This is a first assembly of steps already argued, individually, across
several research notes, into one ordered procedure. It is not a validated protocol. Every step
is marked as either *specified* (a real quantity or condition is given in a source note) or
*unspecified* (the mechanism is argued but no concrete parameter has been chosen). See the
companion bill of materials for reagent identities and hazard flags.

---

## Stage 1 — Base substrate cure (specified in outline, unspecified in exact quantity)

1. Calcine zeolite 4A at 450–550 °C; handle dry thereafter. *(specified — temperature range
   given.)*
2. Combine divinyl-terminated PDMS, PETMP at SH:vinyl above unity, and calcined zeolite
   particles. *(specified direction, unspecified loading fraction and exact SH:vinyl ratio.)*
3. Add radical photoinitiator (DMPA or equivalent). *(unspecified loading.)*
4. Cast or spin-coat onto the electrode-patterned substrate. *(unspecified film thickness.)*
5. Cure under UV (or thermally) to consume all vinyl into thioether. *(specified mechanism,
   unspecified cure dose/time.)*
6. Expose the cured film to air, or a mild oxidant, to convert residual thiol to disulfide.
   *(specified direction, unspecified oxidant identity, dose, or endpoint — the source note
   flags over-oxidation into the sulfinic/sulfonic ladder as a live risk here specifically.)*

**Result:** a floppy, ion-permeable film at baseline φ, with an initial disulfide population,
sitting on the electrode array.

---

## Stage 2 — Structural write/erase (fully specified, from the thermal-cycle report)

1. **Write.** Pass current to raise local temperature to ~140 °C for the write window
   (120–150 °C). φ rises toward the crystallization plateau. *(specified.)*
2. **Read-lock.** Cut current; cool to ambient. Below $T_v$, exchange kinetics freeze and the
   written φ is retained. *(specified.)*
3. **Erase.** Raise local temperature above 175 °C. Dissolution kinetics dominate and φ falls
   back toward baseline. *(specified.)*
4. **Read.** Measure local dynamic modulus (mechanical) and local impedance (electrical) at
   the same site; the two should move in opposition as φ changes. *(specified as a signature,
   unspecified instrumentation — no probe geometry or measurement protocol is given anywhere
   in the source notes.)*

---

## Stage 3 — Local disulfide rewrite (mechanism specified, reagent/dose unspecified)

1. Dope the cured film with a photoacid or photobase generator (PAG/PBG) — no specific PAG/PBG
   compound is named in any source note. *(unspecified reagent.)*
2. Address a spot with UV or a wavelength matched to the PAG/PBG's absorption. *(unspecified
   wavelength, dose, spot size.)*
3. Read local disulfide/thiol ratio by an unspecified means — no assay is named in any source
   note for measuring this directly on the cured film. *(unspecified readout.)*

**Open gap, not filled by any note so far:** there is no specified way to read the exchange-
population state independent of the mechanical/electrical φ readout of Stage 2. This is worth
flagging on its own — building this channel without a way to read it back is exactly the
"filename, manifest claim, or passing local test does not substitute for the effect" problem
named in this repo's own capability-cell discipline, applied to chemistry instead of software.

---

## Stage 4 — Metal–ligand retuning (mechanism specified, procedure unspecified)

1. Formulate a catechol- or histidine-bearing crosslinker into the base network (not yet
   integrated with Stage 1 above in any source note — this is a proposed addition, not a
   confirmed compatible co-formulation).
2. Equilibrate the cured film against a chosen metal salt solution ($\mathrm{ZnCl_2}$,
   $\mathrm{NiCl_2}$, or $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ depending on desired tier) for an unspecified soak
   time.
3. Read exchange rate by stress relaxation (DMA) at the tier of interest. *(method named,
   parameters unspecified.)*

---

## Stage 5 — Growth, feeding, and locomotion (all three: mechanism specified, procedure absent)

1. **Growth.** Immerse in a dilute silicate/aluminate bath (composition, pH, temperature, and
   duration all unspecified) and weigh dry, before and after, against a solvent-uptake control.
2. **Feeding.** Illuminate a catechol–Fe-doped region in the presence of an external electron
   donor (donor compound unspecified) and assay free-thiol concentration before/after against
   a dark control (assay method unspecified).
3. **Locomotion.** Requires an azobenzene-functionalized liquid-crystal actuator region, not
   yet formulated at all — this stage has no procedure, only a cited mechanism from a
   structurally different material system.

---

## What this document is not

This is not a protocols.io-ready procedure. Every "unspecified" marked above is a real
decision that has to be made — usually by running a small screening experiment, sometimes by
simply picking a reasonable literature-precedented value and stating it — before this becomes
something a lab could follow without guessing. The falsification tests already written into
the source notes are the natural first experiments for resolving the "unspecified" items tied
to Stage 2 onward; Stage 1's unspecified items (loading, dose, ratio) don't have falsification
tests written anywhere yet and are the actual next gap.
