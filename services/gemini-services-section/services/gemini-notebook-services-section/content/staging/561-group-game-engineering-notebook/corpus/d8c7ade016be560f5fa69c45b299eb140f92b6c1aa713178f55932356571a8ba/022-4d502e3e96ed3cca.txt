# Bill of Materials: Two-Phase Siloxane–Zeolite Substrate with Multi-Tier Exchange Chemistry

**Status: draft, unverified.** CAS numbers below are given at the author's best recollection,
not looked up against a live chemical registry, and must be independently confirmed against
current supplier SDS documentation before any material is ordered or handled. This document
does not substitute for a Safety Data Sheet for any listed compound. Where a quantity or ratio
is not given, it was not specified in the source research notes and is an open formulation
question, not an oversight in this table.

---

## Core network

| Component | Compound | CAS (unverified) | Role | Quantity / ratio (source) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Matrix precursor | Divinyl-terminated poly(dimethylsiloxane) (vinyl-PDMS) | ~68083-19-2 (grade-dependent; MW-dependent variants exist under other CAS numbers) | Floppy matrix backbone, $T_g \approx -120\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ | Base resin; exact MW/viscosity grade not specified |
| Crosslinker | Pentaerythritol tetrakis(3-mercaptopropionate) (PETMP) | 7575-23-7 | Tetrafunctional thiol, thiol–ene cure, $f=4$ | SH:vinyl **above unity** (excess thiol required — see note below) |
| Radical photoinitiator | 2,2-dimethoxy-2-phenylacetophenone (DMPA), or equivalent Norrish Type I initiator | ~24650-42-8 | Initiates thiol–ene radical cure under UV | Not specified; typical thiol–ene formulations run 0.5–2 wt% — **not confirmed for this system** |
| Oxidant for post-cure disulfide formation | Ambient air, or a mild chemical oxidant (unspecified in source notes) | — | Converts residual thiol to disulfide after cure | Stoichiometry not specified; controls the exchangeable-population fraction and is flagged in the source notes as a live risk of over-oxidation |

**Note on SH:vinyl ratio.** The organosulfur note specifies excess thiol over vinyl so that
thiol–ene consumes all vinyl into permanent thioether, leaving a residual thiol pool for
post-cure oxidation to disulfide. No specific numeric ratio is given anywhere in the source
notes — this is a real open formulation parameter, not merely omitted here.

## Mandatory before any bench work

1. **Independent SDS lookup for every row above**, from the actual supplier and lot, not from
   this document. Nickel salts specifically carry real, serious hazard classifications
   (sensitization, carcinogenicity category) that this table only flags, not documents fully.
2. **Thiol odor and exposure controls.** Multifunctional thiols, PETMP included, are strong-
   smelling and require fume-hood handling; this was never addressed in any prior note.
3. **Zeolite dust handling** during calcination and compounding — respirable particulate
   controls, standard for any powder ceramic handling, not addressed elsewhere in this corpus.
4. **No quantity in this table should be treated as a validated formulation.** Several are
   explicitly marked unspecified; several others (SH:vinyl ratio, photoinitiator loading, bath
   concentrations) are carried from adjacent, established literature for structurally similar
   systems, not measured for this one.
