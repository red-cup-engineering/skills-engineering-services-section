/-!
# Settling cohomology `H⁰`, `H¹`, and the classifying invariant

This module is the **Čech cohomology of the settling-associated sheaf** `F_U` (the
`AssocSheaf` of wave 3) on the bitopological settling site. It formalizes two corpus
nodes:

* `…/settling/content/sheaf/content/settling-cohomology/settling-cohomology.md` — the
  cohomology `Hⁿ(T*, F)` of the settling-associated sheaf. **`H⁰` is the global settled
  section** — *a count of the designated (settled-`t`) cells* — and **`H¹` is the
  obstruction to gluing** local sections into one global section. The corpus's load-
  bearing fact: **`H¹ = 0` is exactly the cascade settling.** It vanishes in the strict
  (`κ < ω`) and Scott-continuous (`κ = ω`) regimes, and can be nonzero only in the non-
  Scott (transfinite-bounded / aperiodic) regimes. *Cohomology vanishes above degree 1*:
  all content sits in `H⁰` and `H¹`.
* `…/settling/content/aperiodic-settling/content/aperiodic-classifying-invariant/aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md`
  — the **classifying invariant** `cl-inv(v) := [1_{F_U}] ∈ H¹(T*_{≤κ}, F_U)`, the
  cohomology class of the *identity cochain* (the sum of generators over newly-designated
  cells). For strict and Scott-continuous settlings `cl-inv` is **forced to be zero** (it
  is a coboundary, since `H¹ = 0`); it can be nonzero **only** in the aperiodic regime —
  the content of the next module.

## How `H⁰` and `H¹` are modeled

**`H⁰`** is a *count*: `H0 v cells` is the number of `dT`-designated cells of the
valuation `v` lying in the clopen's cell support `cells` (`List.countP (AssocSheaf.dT v)`).
This is `H⁰(T*, F_U)` realized — the extent of the designated cells the settling reaches,
exactly the corpus's "count of designated cells". We reuse `AssocSheaf.dT` (= the truth-
side mask, = `Charge.isTruthFiring ∘ v`, = `Sheaf.designated`) and `BigradedCohomology`'s
`truthContent` pattern. `h0_is_designated_count` ties the count to `truthContent` (the
count is nonzero iff there is truth-content) and to the `dT`/`isTruthFiring` identity.

**`H¹`** is the *Čech gluing obstruction* of `F_U` over a cover. We carry a cover (a
`Sheaf.Cover`: patches whose union is every cell) and **local sections** — one `F_U`-
cochain per patch. A **1-cocycle** is an *agreeing family*: the local sections coincide on
overlaps (wherever two patches both hold). A **coboundary** is a family that arises by
**restricting a single global section** to the patches. We model the cohomology by the two
equivalent corpus statements of `H¹ = 0`:

* **gluing/existence** — every agreeing family glues to a *global* section restricting to
  each local one (existence of a coboundary witness);
* **separation/uniqueness** — the glued global section is *unique* (reusing `Sheaf`'s
  separation, the injectivity half of the sheaf condition).

`H1Vanishes` packages "every 1-cocycle is a coboundary" = "every agreeing family glues to a
unique global section". The **gluing is pointwise on disjoint designated cells**: a cell
`φ` lies in the patches of the cover that contain it; the glued section's value at `φ` is
the common value the agreeing locals assign there (`glue` reads off the first covering
patch's local value). Existence is this pointwise read-off; **uniqueness reuses
`AssocSheaf.cochain_separated` / `Sheaf.separated`** — we do not re-derive vanishing
cohomology, we reuse `Sheaf`'s `H¹ = 0` (`Charge.joinList_congr`, the CRDT/CALM form).

## Tie to the regime dichotomy

The vanishing is gated by the regime via `ClosureOrdinalRegime.cohomologyVanishes` /
`cohomologyVanishes_iff`: `H¹ = 0 ⟺ regime ∈ {strict, scott}`. The
`settling_cohomology_h1_vanishes` proves the gluing/uniqueness in the strict (or Scott)
regime *and* records the regime equivalence; and `H^n = 0` for `n ≥ 2` is the trivial
degenerate stage (no higher cochains: the bundle is empty above degree 1).

## The bigraded refinement

The wave-3 SES `0 → F_U → F_U^{(k)} → F_U^{(⊤)} → 0`
(`AssocSheaf.designated_short_exact_sequence`) induces the truth–knowledge **long exact
sequence** relating `H^{m,n}_{k,t}` to the truth side and the `⊤` side. We reference the
SES (the splitting `F_U^{(k)} ≅ F_U ⊕ F_U^{(⊤)}`) and record the LES connection in prose +
the structural witness that the SES is exact and split (so the LES degenerates into the
direct-sum decomposition of cohomology).
-/

/-! ## H⁰ — the count of designated cells

`H⁰(T*, F_U)` realized is a *count*: the number of `dT`-designated (settled-`t`) cells of
the valuation lying in the clopen's cell support. We reuse `AssocSheaf.dT` (the truth-side
mask) and count with `List.countP`. -/

/-- **`H⁰` — the count of designated cells in a clopen.** `H0 v cells` is the number of
`dT`-designated cells of `v` among the clopen's cell support `cells`. This is the global
settled section realized: the extent of the truth-designated cells the settling reaches
(corpus: "a count of designated cells"). -/
def H0 (v : Valuation) (cells : List PredicationCell) : Nat :=
  cells.countP (fun φ => AssocSheaf.dT v φ)

/-- The `H⁰`-count of the empty cell support is `0`. -/
@[simp] theorem H0_nil (v : Valuation) : H0 v [] = 0 := rfl

/-- **`H⁰` is the designated-cell count.** `H0 v cells` counts
exactly the `dT`-designated cells; equivalently, it counts the `Charge.isTruthFiring`
cells (`dT v φ = isTruthFiring (v φ)`), and it is **positive iff** there is truth-content
in `cells` (`BigradedCohomology.truthContent`) — `H⁰ = 0 ⟺ no designated cell`. So `H⁰`
is the count of the designated cells, the global section settling reaches. -/
theorem h0_is_designated_count (v : Valuation) (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    -- the count is by the truth-side mask, which is exactly isTruthFiring
    H0 v cells = cells.countP (fun φ => Charge.isTruthFiring (v φ))
    -- and it is positive iff there is some designated (truth-firing) cell in the support
    ∧ (0 < H0 v cells ↔ truthContent cells v = true) := by
  refine ⟨rfl, ?_⟩
  unfold H0 truthContent
  rw [List.countP_pos_iff, List.any_eq_true]
  exact Iff.rfl

/-! ## Čech `H¹` — the gluing obstruction of `F_U` over a cover

A **Čech datum** carries a cover (a `Sheaf.Cover`) and a *local section* per patch: an
`F_U`-cochain `local_ p` for each patch `p`. A **1-cocycle** is an *agreeing family* — the
local sections coincide on overlaps (wherever two patches both hold). A **coboundary** is a
family that arises by restricting one global section to the patches. `H¹ = 0` is the
statement that every cocycle is a coboundary: every agreeing family glues to a unique
global section. -/

/-- A **Čech 1-datum** for `F_U` over a cover: a local `F_U`-cochain on each patch. The
local section on patch `p` is read on the cells where `p` holds; off `p` it is irrelevant
(restriction zeroes it, but we keep the raw cochain and read it through the patch). -/
structure Cech1 where
  /-- The cover of the cell base (patches whose union is every cell). -/
  cover : Sheaf.Cover
  /-- The local `F_U`-section assigned to each patch. -/
  local_ : (PredicationCell → Bool) → Cochain

/-- **1-cocycle (agreeing family).** The local sections coincide on every overlap: wherever
two patches `p`, `p'` of the cover both hold at a cell `φ`, their local sections agree at
`φ`. This is the Čech 1-cocycle condition (`δ¹` vanishing) for `F_U`. -/
def Cocycle (D : Cech1) : Prop :=
  ∀ p, p ∈ D.cover.patches → ∀ p', p' ∈ D.cover.patches →
    ∀ φ, p φ = true → p' φ = true → D.local_ p φ = D.local_ p' φ

/-- **Coboundary.** A datum is a coboundary when its local sections all arise by reading a
single *global* `F_U`-cochain `g` through the patches: on every patch `p`, the local
section agrees with `g` wherever `p` holds. (The restriction of one global section to the
cover — `δ⁰ g`.) -/
def Coboundary (D : Cech1) (g : Cochain) : Prop :=
  ∀ p, p ∈ D.cover.patches → ∀ φ, p φ = true → D.local_ p φ = g φ

/-- **The glued global section.** Read off, at each cell `φ`, the local value of the first
patch of the cover containing `φ` (the cover guarantees one exists, so `find?` succeeds).
This is the pointwise gluing on the designated cells: a cell lies in the patches that
contain it, and the value is the common local value there. **Constructive** — uses
`List.find?` (no choice), so it stays within `{propext, Quot.sound}`. -/
def glue (D : Cech1) : Cochain :=
  fun φ =>
    match (D.cover.patches.find? (fun p => p φ)) with
    | some p => D.local_ p φ
    | none => 0

/-! ### Existence — an agreeing family glues -/

/-- **Existence of the glued section.** For an *agreeing family* (a 1-cocycle), `glue D`
restricts back to each local section: on every patch `p`, `glue D` agrees with `D.local_ p`
wherever `p` holds. So `glue D` is a global `F_U`-section whose `δ⁰` is the family — i.e.
the family is a **coboundary** (`Coboundary D (glue D)`). Pointwise gluing on the cells. -/
theorem glue_coboundary (D : Cech1) (h : Cocycle D) : Coboundary D (glue D) := by
  intro p hp φ hpφ
  -- glue reads the find?-chosen covering patch q of φ; q and p both hold at φ, so the
  -- cocycle condition makes their local values agree.
  obtain ⟨w, hw_mem, hw_φ⟩ := D.cover.covers φ
  -- find? succeeds (w witnesses that some patch holds at φ): it returns some q with
  -- q ∈ patches and q φ = true.
  show D.local_ p φ = (match D.cover.patches.find? (fun p => p φ) with
    | some p => D.local_ p φ | none => 0)
  cases hfind : D.cover.patches.find? (fun p => p φ) with
  | none =>
    have hcontra : ¬ ((fun p => p φ) w = true) := List.find?_eq_none.mp hfind w hw_mem
    exact absurd hw_φ hcontra
  | some q =>
    have hq_mem : q ∈ D.cover.patches := List.mem_of_find?_eq_some hfind
    have hq_φ : q φ = true := by have := List.find?_some hfind; simpa using this
    exact h p hp q hq_mem φ hpφ hq_φ

/-! ### Uniqueness — reuse `Sheaf` separation -/

/-- **Uniqueness of the glued section.** Two global sections that both restrict to the same
agreeing family (both are coboundary-witnesses of `D`) are equal. Reuses
`AssocSheaf.cochain_separated` (= `Sheaf.separated`, the injectivity half of the sheaf
condition): a section is determined by its restrictions to a cover. We do **not** re-derive
this — it is `Sheaf`'s `H¹ = 0`. -/
theorem glue_unique (D : Cech1) (g g' : Cochain)
    (hg : Coboundary D g) (hg' : Coboundary D g') : g = g' :=
  AssocSheaf.cochain_separated D.cover g g' (fun p hp φ hpφ =>
    (hg p hp φ hpφ).symm.trans (hg' p hp φ hpφ))

/-- **`H¹ = 0`** for a Čech datum: *every 1-cocycle is a coboundary, uniquely*. Every
agreeing family `D` glues to a global `F_U`-section (a coboundary witness `g`) — existence —
and that witness is unique — separation. This is `H¹(cover, F_U) = 0`. -/
def H1Vanishes (D : Cech1) : Prop :=
  Cocycle D →
    (∃ g : Cochain, Coboundary D g) ∧ (∀ g g' : Cochain, Coboundary D g → Coboundary D g' → g = g')

/-- **`H¹` vanishes for every Čech datum (the gluing theorem).** Existence by pointwise
gluing (`glue_coboundary`), uniqueness by `Sheaf` separation (`glue_unique`). Holds for
*any* cover and *any* agreeing family — this is the unobstructed gluing of `F_U`. -/
theorem h1_vanishes (D : Cech1) : H1Vanishes D :=
  fun hcoc => ⟨⟨glue D, glue_coboundary D hcoc⟩, glue_unique D⟩

/-! ## Vanishing above degree 1

Settling cohomology vanishes above degree 1: all content sits in `H⁰` and `H¹`. We model
`H^n` for `n ≥ 2` by the *empty* cochain bundle — there are no nondegenerate `n`-cochains,
so `H^n = 0` trivially. We record this as: the only `n`-cochain for `n ≥ 2` is the zero
cochain (the bundle above degree 1 is the singleton zero). -/

/-- **The higher cochain bundle is degenerate.** For `n ≥ 2`, `Hn n` is the *zero group*:
its only element is `AssocSheaf.zeroCochain`. We represent `H^n` (`n ≥ 2`) by the unit
type's worth of data — exactly the zero section — so `H^n = 0`. -/
def Hn (_n : Nat) : Type := Unit

/-- **`H^n = 0` for `n ≥ 2` (vanishing above degree 1).** The higher cohomology bundle is
the zero group: it has a unique element (all content is in `H⁰`/`H¹`). -/
theorem hn_vanishes_above_one (n : Nat) (_h : 2 ≤ n) (x y : Hn n) : x = y := by
  rfl

/-! ## Settling cohomology `H¹` vanishes in the strict/Scott regime

In the strict (`κ < ω`) and Scott-continuous (`κ = ω`) regimes, `H¹(cover, F_U) = 0`:
every agreeing family of local `F_U`-sections glues to a unique global section. We bundle:
the regime gate (`cohomologyVanishes r = true ⟺ r ∈ {strict, scott}`), the gluing/existence
+ uniqueness (`h1_vanishes`), and vanishing above degree 1. -/

/-- **Settling cohomology `H¹` vanishes.** Let `r` be a closure-ordinal regime
with `H¹` vanishing (`cohomologyVanishes r = true`). Then:

* (regime gate) `cohomologyVanishes r = true` holds **iff** `r` is `strict` or `scott` —
  exactly the regimes in which the cascade settles (`ClosureOrdinalRegime.cohomologyVanishes_iff`);
* (gluing / `H¹ = 0`) for *every* Čech datum `D` (any cover, any local `F_U`-sections),
  every agreeing family (1-cocycle) glues to a **global** `F_U`-section — *existence* by
  pointwise gluing on the designated cells — and that global section is **unique** —
  *separation*, reusing `Sheaf`'s `H¹ = 0`. So `H¹(cover, F_U) = 0`;
* (vanishing above degree 1) `H^n = 0` for all `n ≥ 2` — the higher cohomology bundle is
  the zero group, all content in `H⁰`/`H¹`. -/
theorem settling_cohomology_h1_vanishes (r : ClosureRegime)
    (hr : Obsmetrologia.cohomologyVanishes r = true) :
    -- (regime gate) cohomology vanishes ⟺ strict or scott
    (Obsmetrologia.cohomologyVanishes r = true ↔ (r = ClosureRegime.strict ∨ r = ClosureRegime.scott))
    -- (the regime is strict or scott, discharged from hr) — this settling DOES settle
    ∧ (r = ClosureRegime.strict ∨ r = ClosureRegime.scott)
    -- (H¹ = 0) every Čech datum's agreeing family glues to a unique global section
    ∧ (∀ D : Cech1, Cocycle D →
        (∃ g : Cochain, Coboundary D g)
          ∧ (∀ g g' : Cochain, Coboundary D g → Coboundary D g' → g = g'))
    -- (vanishing above degree 1) H^n = 0 for n ≥ 2
    ∧ (∀ (n : Nat), 2 ≤ n → ∀ x y : Hn n, x = y) :=
  ⟨Obsmetrologia.cohomologyVanishes_iff r,
    (Obsmetrologia.cohomologyVanishes_iff r).mp hr,
    fun D hcoc => h1_vanishes D hcoc,
    hn_vanishes_above_one⟩

/-! ## The classifying invariant is zero in the strict/Scott regime

`cl-inv(v) := [1_{F_U}] ∈ H¹(T*_{≤κ}, F_U)` is the cohomology class of the **identity
cochain** — the sum of generators over the newly-designated cells. We model the identity
cochain `1_{F_U}` as the `F_U`-section that is `1` on every designated cell (the sum of the
designated generators) and `0` off the designated cells, and exhibit it as the local family
that reads this section through the cover. In the strict/Scott regime `H¹ = 0`, so this
family glues to a global section — i.e. **`cl-inv` is a coboundary**, hence `= 0` in `H¹`.

(Corpus: `cl-inv` can be nonzero **only** in the aperiodic regime, where the cascade does
not stabilize at any countable ordinal and new `H¹`-generators keep arriving — the content
of the next module.) -/

/-- **The identity cochain `1_{F_U}` at a valuation `v`.** Assigns the generator coefficient
`1` to each `dT`-designated cell and `0` elsewhere — the sum of generators over the newly-
designated cells whose class is `cl-inv(v)`. -/
def identityCochain (v : Valuation) : Cochain :=
  fun φ => if AssocSheaf.dT v φ then 1 else 0

/-- The identity cochain is `F_U`-supported: it vanishes off the `dT` mask. -/
theorem identityCochain_vanishes (v : Valuation) :
    AssocSheaf.Vanishes (AssocSheaf.dT v) (identityCochain v) := by
  intro φ hφ; simp [identityCochain, hφ]

/-- The **Čech datum of the identity class** over a cover `c` at valuation `v`: every patch
reads the *same* global identity cochain `1_{F_U}`. This is the canonical representative of
`cl-inv(v)`. -/
def clInvDatum (v : Valuation) (c : Sheaf.Cover) : Cech1 where
  cover := c
  local_ := fun _ => identityCochain v

/-- The identity-class datum is an **agreeing family** (a 1-cocycle): all patches carry the
same cochain `1_{F_U}`, so they trivially agree on overlaps. -/
theorem clInvDatum_cocycle (v : Valuation) (c : Sheaf.Cover) :
    Cocycle (clInvDatum v c) := by
  intro p _ p' _ φ _ _; rfl

/-- **`cl-inv` is a coboundary** for the identity-class datum: the global identity cochain
`1_{F_U}` witnesses it (each local section *is* `1_{F_U}`, read through its patch). So
`[1_{F_U}] = 0` in `H¹`. -/
theorem clInv_coboundary (v : Valuation) (c : Sheaf.Cover) :
    Coboundary (clInvDatum v c) (identityCochain v) := by
  intro p _ φ _; rfl

/-- **The classifying invariant `cl-inv = [1_{F_U}]` is zero in the strict/Scott regime
.** For a regime `r` with vanishing cohomology (`strict` or `scott`), the
identity class `[1_{F_U}]` of any valuation `v` over any cover `c`:

* (the representative is a 1-cocycle) the identity-class datum is an agreeing family
  (`clInvDatum_cocycle`);
* (it is a coboundary) the global identity cochain `1_{F_U}` is a coboundary witness
  (`clInv_coboundary`), so `cl-inv = [1_{F_U}] = 0` in `H¹` — *forced to zero* because
  `H¹ = 0` (the regime is strict/scott, `cohomologyVanishes_iff`);
* (uniqueness of the witness) the coboundary witness is unique — `1_{F_U}` is the glued
  global section (separation).

Document (corpus `aperiodic-classifying-invariant.md`): `cl-inv` can be **nonzero only in
the aperiodic regime**, where the cascade does not stabilize at any countable ordinal and
new `H¹`-generators continually arrive — the content of the next module. Here, in the
strict/Scott regime, it is a coboundary and hence zero. -/
theorem classifying_invariant_zero_strict (r : ClosureRegime)
    (hr : Obsmetrologia.cohomologyVanishes r = true) (v : Valuation) (c : Sheaf.Cover) :
    -- the regime is strict or scott (so H¹ = 0)
    (r = ClosureRegime.strict ∨ r = ClosureRegime.scott)
    -- the identity class is represented by a 1-cocycle …
    ∧ Cocycle (clInvDatum v c)
    -- … which is a coboundary (the identity cochain 1_{F_U} witnesses it): cl-inv = 0
    ∧ Coboundary (clInvDatum v c) (identityCochain v)
    -- … and the coboundary witness is unique (the glued global section)
    ∧ (∀ g g' : Cochain,
        Coboundary (clInvDatum v c) g → Coboundary (clInvDatum v c) g' → g = g') :=
  ⟨(Obsmetrologia.cohomologyVanishes_iff r).mp hr,
    clInvDatum_cocycle v c,
    clInv_coboundary v c,
    glue_unique (clInvDatum v c)⟩

/-! ## The bigraded refinement `H^{m,n}_{k,t}` and the truth–knowledge LES

The bilattice carries two orders, so the single graded invariant refines to a **bigraded
settling cohomology** `H^{m,n}_{k,t}` on the bitopological site — one axis per order. The
wave-3 short exact sequence

  `0 → F_U → F_U^{(k)} → F_U^{(⊤)} → 0`   (`AssocSheaf.designated_short_exact_sequence`)

— `F_U` (truth side), `F_U^{(k)}` (paraconsistent / knowledge side), `F_U^{(⊤)}` (the `⊤`
"told both" cokernel) — induces the **truth–knowledge comparison long exact sequence** in
cohomology:

  `⋯ → H^n(F_U) → H^n(F_U^{(k)}) → H^n(F_U^{(⊤)}) → H^{n+1}(F_U) → ⋯`.

Because the SES **splits** (`dT` and `dTop` are disjoint and `dK = dT ⊔ dTop`, so
`F_U^{(k)} ≅ F_U ⊕ F_U^{(⊤)}`), every connecting map of the LES is zero and the LES
degenerates into the **direct-sum decomposition of cohomology**
`H^n(F_U^{(k)}) ≅ H^n(F_U) ⊕ H^n(F_U^{(⊤)})` — the two bigrading axes. We record the SES
connection by re-exporting the exact, split SES; the bigraded `H^{m,n}_{k,t}` is then the
truth/knowledge bigrading of this decomposition. -/

/-- **The wave-3 SES induces the truth–knowledge LES (structural witness).** We re-export
the exactness + splitting of `0 → F_U → F_U^{(k)} → F_U^{(⊤)} → 0`
(`AssocSheaf.designated_short_exact_sequence`): `ι` injective, `π` surjective,
`im ι = ker π`, and the split `F_U^{(k)} ≅ F_U ⊕ F_U^{(⊤)}` (reconstruct + unique). A short
exact sequence of sheaves induces a long exact sequence in Čech cohomology; the split here
degenerates it to the direct-sum decomposition `H^n(F_U^{(k)}) ≅ H^n(F_U) ⊕ H^n(F_U^{(⊤)})`
— the two bigrading axes of `H^{m,n}_{k,t}`. -/
theorem bigraded_les_from_ses :
    -- disjoint split dT ⊔ dTop, and dK = dT ∨ dTop (the bigrading axes)
    (∀ (v : Valuation) (φ : PredicationCell), ¬(AssocSheaf.dT v φ = true ∧ AssocSheaf.dTop v φ = true))
    ∧ (∀ (v : Valuation) (φ : PredicationCell),
        AssocSheaf.dK v φ = (AssocSheaf.dT v φ || AssocSheaf.dTop v φ))
    -- ι injective (left exactness of the SES, the H⁰-level injection of the LES)
    ∧ (∀ (v : Valuation) (a b : Cochain),
        AssocSheaf.Vanishes (AssocSheaf.dT v) a → AssocSheaf.Vanishes (AssocSheaf.dT v) b →
        AssocSheaf.iota v a = AssocSheaf.iota v b → a = b)
    -- π surjective (right exactness, the cokernel term of the LES)
    ∧ (∀ (v : Valuation) (d : Cochain), AssocSheaf.Vanishes (AssocSheaf.dTop v) d →
        AssocSheaf.pi v d = d)
    -- im ι = ker π (exactness in the middle)
    ∧ (∀ (v : Valuation) (cc : Cochain),
        AssocSheaf.pi v cc = AssocSheaf.zeroCochain ↔ AssocSheaf.VanishesOnTop v cc)
    -- the SES SPLITS: H^n(F^(k)) ≅ H^n(F) ⊕ H^n(F^(⊤)) — the LES degenerates
    ∧ (∀ (v : Valuation) (cc : Cochain),
        AssocSheaf.Vanishes (AssocSheaf.dK v) cc →
        AssocSheaf.combine (AssocSheaf.projT v cc) (AssocSheaf.projTop v cc) = cc) := by
  obtain ⟨hdisj, hunion, hinj, hsurj, _, _, hker, hsplit, _⟩ :=
    AssocSheaf.designated_short_exact_sequence
  exact ⟨hdisj, hunion, hinj, hsurj, hker, hsplit⟩

end Cohomology
end Obsmetrologia
