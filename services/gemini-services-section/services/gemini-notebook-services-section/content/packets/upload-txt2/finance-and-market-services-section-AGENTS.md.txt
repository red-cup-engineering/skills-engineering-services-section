# Finance and Market Services Section

This sovereign Executive Office service section observes supplied commercial
evidence. It does not own the counterparties, their books, their prices, their
assets, or the Union market. The chief financial officer governs the
accounting product; the investment review board and integrated portfolio
management team retain allocation and continuation decisions.

Every request crosses this membrane as ActivityStreams and contains exact
party-supplied evidence. The section never crawls a settlement tree. It writes
only its own dated `data/` records and returns observations for each receiver
to admit or refuse independently.

The section also maintains one finite advisory referral catalog for exactly
five parent contract boundaries plus one independently admitted child boundary.
A caller may ask the existing section actor one exact, named intent and receive
the pinned actor, contract, operations, and evidence needed to approach that
boundary. The answer is an observed interface referral, not a provider offer,
capability, availability claim, invocation, price, contract, settlement, or
endorsement. A child is referable only through a Finance-owned exact admission
record whose foreign carriers are rechecked offline. The referral runtime reads
only Finance-owned carriers; it never resolves, reads, imports, executes, or
writes the child settlement. No later child is admitted by analogy.

Its first contract reconciles one buyer book, one seller book, their receipt
copies, the buyer transaction, and the seller fulfillment. It separates
fulfillment evidence from value captured, explicitly leaves end-user value
unknown, preserves unknown and contested facts, and may report what
continuation evidence remains absent. It may not mint a
currency, impose a price, allocate resources, set a distribution formula,
write a counterparty's book, or turn its observations into a global ledger.

Its economic market primitive payload is one `CommodityUtterance`: the ordered
`(token, claim)` pair. It must not be called the formal Obsmetrologia bit:
that is the `Bool × Bool` articulation status in a separate
`WitnessBitProduction`, the producer's witnessing work and illocution over one
payload. A replayable content-addressed byte carrier and finite
`CommodityUtteranceTrace` carry payloads; a producer may present these with
their production records as a `CommodityUtteranceLot`. Perlocution/customer
effect is a later counterparty-owned observation, never a mutation or field of
the sold payload or its production. The section assays this form against a named local
counterstate and returns a FOUR-valued evaluation through a proof-bearing
Hoare traversal. This is an Executive Office market protocol derived from
Obsmetrologia's existing `Utterance`, articulation-cell, `Trace`, and
it-to-bit-to-byte readings. It is not a new mathematical primitive or a claim
that the application is a proved Chu construction.

An assay does not make a bit globally fungible, grant title, set price, or glue
the producer's claim into another territory. Those claims can arise only at a
counterparty border under a separate offer and contract. The assay preserves
token, claim, producer, witness actor, and bit identity as protected
distinctions. Application-level outputs such as readings, plans, and reports
are specialized productions over commodity-utterance traces; a service label alone is
not primitive commodity evidence.

Border price discovery compares one seller-owned ask and one buyer-owned bid
bound to the same exact lot digest. The section may expose an incomparable
pair, an open quote gap, an exact match, or a crossed range. It never selects
within a crossed range: only the counterparties may accept one common terms
hash and form a contract. Quote comparison is local defect discovery at one
declared seam, not an order book, global price system, or authority to relax a
protected jurisdictional distinction.

The child border-price quote formation service is the issuer-facing boundary
before comparison. It formats and assays an exact ask or bid only when the
ActivityStreams actor is the issuer and the issuer supplies the complete lot,
side, counterparty, consideration, validity, and a terms-bound authority
record. Finance remains formatter and assayer rather than issuer; its result
is not publication, delivery, counterparty acceptance, price selection,
contract, title, trade, settlement, book entry, x402 receipt, or customer
effect.

The child customer-effect observation formation service is the customer-facing
boundary after settlement evidence exists. It formats and structurally assays
an exact observation candidate only when the ActivityStreams actor, observer,
and receipt buyer are the same customer and customer-held authority binds the
complete lot, receipt, outcome, effect utterance, evidence, time, and intended
use. Finance remains formatter and assayer rather than observer. Its result is
not an effect commodity lot, truth judgment, publication, settlement, party
book entry, customer mutation, end-user-value finding, or reinvestment
authorization.

The child economic-frontier composition service joins only a caller-declared,
finite, digest-bound set of provider-authored local cycle observations. It
preserves every local `firstAbsent` and performs an attributed FOUR knowledge
join without claiming that the denominator is the Union. Capability observed
at another border is reported separately and cannot advance the focal lot;
outside-denominator state remains unknown.

Two distinct, fully replayable bilateral evidence bundles establish only
repeated supplier performance: quote, receipt, books, fulfillment, and a
counterparty effect observation. They do not establish Rendanheyi user value,
zero distance, or reinvestment eligibility. Those claims additionally require
an attributable end user, one chain-group contract with an outcome and target,
value-added accounting, and evidence from the holder of distribution rights.
The section may report these absences; it cannot allocate, set distribution,
or promote pair-local consideration to a Union denomination.

A2A is reserved for coordination among future offices or services inside this
section. Counterparty traffic is ActivityStreams; network ActivityPub is not
claimed until deployed.
