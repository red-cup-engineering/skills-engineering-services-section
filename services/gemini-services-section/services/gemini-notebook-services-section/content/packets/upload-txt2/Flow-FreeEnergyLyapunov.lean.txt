/-!
# Free energy as a Lyapunov function — the H-theorem of settling

This module identifies the corpus's closure settling with gradient flow of a free energy
`F = E − T·S`, proves the H-theorem (a monotone potential strictly decreasing to the
unique fixed point), reads the spectral gap / convergence rate off the initial free
energy, and ties `H¹ = 0 ⟺ unique equilibrium` to a constructive energy estimate.
Rendered over `Nat` and the knowledge-value order; reals are available
(`Obsmetrologia.Real.Internal`) but not needed, since the value order is the preference.

It formalizes two corpus nodes under the *knowledge-least valuation* settling subtree:

* `…/settling/_archive/predication-…-utility-driven-closure-dynamics.md` — the contrast
  *closure-driven vs strategy-driven* settling. The corpus chooses (7a) to layer
  preference **on the bilattice values**: agent preference is the residuated value order
  `≤ₖ` itself, not an external real utility. So the natural Lyapunov potential is built
  from the **knowledge-order height** of charges, and the four-regime dichotomy
  *stratifies the equilibria* — *strict-regime games have an immediate equilibrium*.
  This is the **`T → 0` (Boolean / Datalog hard-closure) limit**: the rules fire
  deterministically (`closureAtZeroTemp`), no stochastic preference weighting.
* `…/settling/_archive/predication-…-closure-flow-curvature-regime-correspondence.md` —
  *entropy is the analytic invariant of the closure-flow curvature `κ`*, and `H¹` is the
  curvature module. In the **flat** (strict / Scott) regimes `H¹ = 0`, the flow has no
  curvature events, and the equilibrium is **unique**. We make the analytic invariant
  concrete: the **free-energy deficit** is a `Nat` Lyapunov function whose strict descent
  to `0` is the H-theorem; `κ` (the firing-step count) is the **ordinal invariant**, the
  energy its **analytic invariant**, and `κ ≤ Φ 0`.

## The Lyapunov potential (all `Nat`-valued over a finite cell list)

The closure orbit **ascends** in `≤ₖ` toward its fixed point `w` (= the settled
valuation / lfp). So the natural Lyapunov potential is the **deficit to the fixed point**:

  `kHeight a` — the knowledge-order height of a charge (`kBot ↦ 0`, `tTop,tBot ↦ 1`,
  `kTop ↦ 2`), the distance up to `⊤ₖ`; `KLE a b → kHeight a ≤ kHeight b`.

  `deficit cells v w := Σ_{φ ∈ cells} (kHeight (w φ) − kHeight (v φ))`  (`Nat` subtraction)

— how far `v` sits *below* the settled `w`, summed over the finite support `cells`. It is
`≥ 0` trivially (`Nat`), `= 0` iff `v` and `w` have equal height on every support cell
(equilibrium reached on the support), and **antitone along the orbit** toward `w`: each
inflationary step `orbit n ≤ₖ orbit (n+1) ≤ₖ w` weakly decreases it. A **firing** — a
step that actually changes a support cell — drops it by **≥ 1**: the free energy strictly
decreases. Since it is a `Nat` bounded below by `0`, the orbit reaches the fixed point in
**`≤ Φ 0`** firing steps — the initial free energy bounds the convergence time (the
spectral-gap estimate), and equals the bound on the closure ordinal `κ`.

## The stage ledger

The H-theorem's inequality is the shadow of a conservation law: `ledger_step` (energy at
`v` = step flux + energy at `v'` for an ascending sandwich), `ledger_total` (seed energy =
flux so far + residue, at every horizon), `ledger_closes` (the book closes at quiescence),
`stepFlux_decomposition` (flux = commitments + mints + 2·jumps, per-cell law axiom-free),
and `truth_loss_is_record_gain` (truth losses are record gains — the quench, counted).
Bundled as `stage_ledger`.

## Faithfulness / scope (substitutions)

* Rendered over `Nat` **by design**: `F = E − T·S` is the integer reading. `kHeight` is the integer knowledge-order
  height; the deficit is its `Nat`-summed gap. We do **not** fabricate a real-valued
  entropy `S`; the `T → 0` limit (hard Boolean closure) is the only regime where the
  potential is purely the energy `E` (no entropic term), and that is the strict/Scott
  spine this whole Obsmetrologia front lives at. `closureAtZeroTemp` exhibits the hard closure.
* Finite support: like `SettlingCohomologyH1.H0`, the potential is a sum over an explicit
  `cells : List PredicationCell` — the clopen support the settling reaches. The
  strict-decrease/convergence statements are relative to a support that contains the cells
  that change (stated as hypotheses), exactly the finite-support pattern of `H⁰`.
* `w` is taken to be a charge-closure fixed point (`liftOp c.op w = w`) reachable as the
  pointwise-lift lfp (`ClosureOperatorFiber.settleLift`), so the orbit toward it is the settling orbit of `ClosureOperatorFiber.iterV`.
-/

/-! ## (1) Per-cell knowledge-order height

The knowledge order on `Charge` is the height-graded bilattice `⊥ₖ < {t, f} < ⊤ₖ`.
`kHeight a` is the distance from `⊥ₖ` up to `a` — `0` at the seed `⊥ₖ`, `1` at the two
truth poles `t`/`f`, `2` at the held contradiction `⊤ₖ`. Climbing the knowledge order
weakly increases height; a *strict* climb (a covering step `a < b`) increases it by ≥1.
This is the integer "energy level" of a charge — the analytic invariant of its settling
distance. -/

/-- The **knowledge-order height** of a charge: its distance up the `≤ₖ` order from the
seed `⊥ₖ`. `kBot ↦ 0`, `tTop ↦ 1`, `tBot ↦ 1`, `kTop ↦ 2`. -/
def kHeight : Charge → Nat
  | Articulating.Corner.potential => 0   -- kBot
  | Articulating.Corner.active => 1      -- tTop
  | Articulating.Corner.stable => 1      -- tBot
  | Articulating.Corner.absurd => 2      -- kTop

@[simp] theorem kHeight_kBot : kHeight kBot = 0 := rfl
@[simp] theorem kHeight_kTop : kHeight kTop = 2 := rfl
@[simp] theorem kHeight_tTop : kHeight tTop = 1 := rfl
@[simp] theorem kHeight_tBot : kHeight tBot = 1 := rfl

/-- **The knowledge order increases height.** `KLE a b → kHeight a ≤ kHeight b`: climbing
`≤ₖ` never lowers the energy level. Proven by exhaustion on the finite charge. -/
theorem kHeight_mono {a b : Charge} (h : KLE a b) : kHeight a ≤ kHeight b := by
  revert h; cases a <;> cases b <;> decide

/-- **A strict knowledge climb strictly raises height.** If `a ≤ₖ b` and `a ≠ b`, then
`kHeight a < kHeight b`: a covering step in the knowledge order is a firing that
adds at least one unit of height. (`tTop`/`tBot` are `≤ₖ`-incomparable, so distinct equal-
height charges are never `≤ₖ`-related — this is why a strict climb is a strict rise.) -/
theorem kHeight_lt_of_lt {a b : Charge} (hle : KLE a b) (hne : a ≠ b) :
    kHeight a < kHeight b := by
  revert hle hne; cases a <;> cases b <;> decide

/-! ## (2) The free-energy deficit — where energy is first defined

`deficit cells v w` sums, over the finite support `cells`, the per-cell height gap
`kHeight (w φ) − kHeight (v φ)` (`Nat` truncated subtraction). It measures how far `v`
sits *below* the settled value `w` in total knowledge-order height — the free energy `F`
to be minimized along the flow toward `w`.

This is the first mathematical carrier for **energy** in this front: not a named constant
or side definition, but the Lyapunov deficit whose monotonicity, strict firing decrease,
zero criterion, and convergence bound are proved below. Bounded below by `0` (`Nat`);
zero exactly when `v` reaches `w`'s height on every support cell. -/

/-- The **free-energy deficit** of `v` relative to the settled value `w` over a finite
support `cells`: `Σ_{φ ∈ cells} (kHeight (w φ) − kHeight (v φ))`. This is where
**energy** is defined as a theorem-bearing quantity: the `Nat`-valued free energy `F`
(the energy `E` in the `T → 0` hard-closure limit), the distance from `v` up to the fixed
point, the Lyapunov potential of the settling flow. -/
def deficit (cells : List PredicationCell) (v w : Valuation) : Nat :=
  cells.foldr (fun φ acc => (kHeight (w φ) - kHeight (v φ)) + acc) 0

@[simp] theorem deficit_nil (v w : Valuation) : deficit [] v w = 0 := rfl

@[simp] theorem deficit_cons (φ : PredicationCell) (cells : List PredicationCell)
    (v w : Valuation) :
    deficit (φ :: cells) v w = (kHeight (w φ) - kHeight (v φ)) + deficit cells v w := rfl

/-- **The free energy is bounded below by `0`** — trivially, being a `Nat`. The flow
cannot overshoot the fixed point. -/
theorem deficit_nonneg (cells : List PredicationCell) (v w : Valuation) :
    0 ≤ deficit cells v w := Nat.zero_le _

/-! ### Antitonicity of the deficit (the H-theorem, weak form)

If `v ≤ₖ v' ≤ₖ w` then every per-cell gap to `w` weakly shrinks (`kHeight (v φ) ≤
kHeight (v' φ)` so `kHeight (w φ) − kHeight (v' φ) ≤ kHeight (w φ) − kHeight (v φ)`),
hence the summed deficit weakly shrinks. Free energy decreases along the ascending flow. -/

/-- **The deficit is antitone in the lower valuation.** If `v ≤ₖ v'` (both heading toward
`w`), then `deficit cells v' w ≤ deficit cells v w`: advancing the orbit one step weakly
decreases the free energy. Proven by list induction, per cell via `Nat.sub_le_sub_left`. -/
theorem deficit_antitone (cells : List PredicationCell) {v v' w : Valuation}
    (h : leK v v') : deficit cells v' w ≤ deficit cells v w := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => simp
  | cons φ rest ih =>
    simp only [deficit_cons]
    have hcell : kHeight (w φ) - kHeight (v' φ) ≤ kHeight (w φ) - kHeight (v φ) :=
      Nat.sub_le_sub_left (kHeight_mono (h φ)) _
    exact Nat.add_le_add hcell ih

/-! ## (3) Strict decrease = firing, and `Φ = 0 ⟺ equilibrium`

A *firing* is a settling step that actually raises a support cell. We isolate the per-cell
strict drop and lift it to the deficit: if a support cell `φ ∈ cells` strictly climbs
(`v φ ≠ v' φ` with `v ≤ₖ v' ≤ₖ w`), the deficit strictly decreases. And `Φ = 0` exactly
when `v` has reached `w`'s height on every support cell — equilibrium on the support. -/

/-- If, over the support, `v ≤ₖ v'` everywhere **and** some support cell `φ` strictly
climbs (`v φ ≠ v' φ`), then the deficit **strictly** drops: `deficit cells v' w < deficit
cells v w`. A firing costs the free energy at least one unit. The strict drop comes from
`kHeight_lt_of_lt` at `φ`; the rest of the sum is weakly antitone (`deficit_antitone`). -/
theorem deficit_strict_of_fires (cells : List PredicationCell) {v v' w : Valuation}
    (hle : leK v v') (hlew : leK v' w) (φ : PredicationCell) (hmem : φ ∈ cells)
    (hne : v φ ≠ v' φ) : deficit cells v' w < deficit cells v w := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => exact absurd hmem (List.not_mem_nil)
  | cons ψ rest ih =>
    simp only [deficit_cons]
    rcases List.mem_cons.1 hmem with hψ | hrest
    · -- the firing cell is the head: strict drop here, weak antitone on the tail
      subst hψ
      have hlt : kHeight (v φ) < kHeight (v' φ) := kHeight_lt_of_lt (hle φ) hne
      have hv'w : kHeight (v' φ) ≤ kHeight (w φ) := kHeight_mono (hlew φ)
      have hcelllt : kHeight (w φ) - kHeight (v' φ) < kHeight (w φ) - kHeight (v φ) := by
        omega
      have htail : deficit rest v' w ≤ deficit rest v w := deficit_antitone rest hle
      omega
    · -- the firing cell is in the tail: weak antitone on the head, strict drop in tail
      have hhead : kHeight (w ψ) - kHeight (v' ψ) ≤ kHeight (w ψ) - kHeight (v ψ) :=
        Nat.sub_le_sub_left (kHeight_mono (hle ψ)) _
      have htail : deficit rest v' w < deficit rest v w := ih hrest
      omega

/-- **`Φ = 0 ⟺ height-equilibrium on the support.** The deficit is zero exactly when `v`
matches `w`'s knowledge-order height on every support cell — the settling has reached the
fixed point's energy level throughout the support. Proven by list induction (`Nat` sum is
zero iff every summand is). -/
theorem deficit_eq_zero_iff (cells : List PredicationCell) (v w : Valuation) :
    deficit cells v w = 0 ↔ ∀ φ ∈ cells, kHeight (w φ) ≤ kHeight (v φ) := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => simp
  | cons φ rest ih =>
    simp only [deficit_cons, Nat.add_eq_zero_iff, Nat.sub_eq_zero_iff_le]
    rw [ih]
    constructor
    · rintro ⟨hφ, hrest⟩ ψ hmem
      rcases List.mem_cons.1 hmem with rfl | h
      · exact hφ
      · exact hrest ψ h
    · intro h
      exact ⟨h φ (List.mem_cons_self), fun ψ hψ => h ψ (List.mem_cons_of_mem _ hψ)⟩

/-- **`Φ = 0` at a fixed point means equilibrium reached on the support.** If `w` is `≤ₖ`
above `v` on the support and `deficit cells v w = 0`, then `v` and `w` have equal height on
every support cell. (When `v ≤ₖ w`, height equilibrium is exactly equality of height — the
two-sided `≤` collapses.) -/
theorem equilibrium_of_deficit_zero (cells : List PredicationCell) {v w : Valuation}
    (hvw : leK v w) (h0 : deficit cells v w = 0) :
    ∀ φ ∈ cells, kHeight (v φ) = kHeight (w φ) := by
  intro φ hmem
  have hle : kHeight (v φ) ≤ kHeight (w φ) := kHeight_mono (hvw φ)
  have hge : kHeight (w φ) ≤ kHeight (v φ) := (deficit_eq_zero_iff cells v w).1 h0 φ hmem
  omega

/-! ## (4) The Lyapunov potential along the pointwise-closure orbit

We instantiate the abstract deficit at the settling orbit: `iterV (liftOp c.op)`
ascending from `seed` toward the pointwise lfp `w := settleLift c seed = liftOp c.op seed`.
`Φ n := deficit cells (iterV (liftOp c.op) n seed) w` is the free energy at settle-time `n`. -/

/-- The settling **orbit** of the pointwise charge-closure from a seed: `clⁿ(seed)` with
`cl = liftOp c.op`. (Same shape as `ClosureOperatorFiber.iterV`, the fiberwise
settling.) -/
def fenOrbit (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) (n : Nat) : Valuation :=
  iterV (liftOp c.op) n seed

@[simp] theorem fenOrbit_zero (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) :
    fenOrbit c seed 0 = seed := rfl

@[simp] theorem fenOrbit_succ (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) (n : Nat) :
    fenOrbit c seed (n + 1) = liftOp c.op (fenOrbit c seed n) := rfl

/-- One orbit step **ascends**: `fenOrbit n ≤ₖ fenOrbit (n+1)` — each closure step is
inflationary, so the free energy is heading downhill (toward the fixed point). -/
theorem fenOrbit_le_succ (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) (n : Nat) :
    leK (fenOrbit c seed n) (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1)) :=
  liftOp_infl c (fenOrbit c seed n)

/-- The orbit is **`≤ₖ`-monotone**: `m ≤ n → fenOrbit m ≤ₖ fenOrbit n`. -/
theorem fenOrbit_monotone (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) {m n : Nat} (h : m ≤ n) :
    leK (fenOrbit c seed m) (fenOrbit c seed n) := by
  induction n with
  | zero => have : m = 0 := Nat.le_zero.1 h; subst this; exact leK_refl _
  | succ k ih =>
    rcases Nat.lt_or_ge m (k + 1) with hlt | hge
    · exact leK_trans (ih (Nat.le_of_lt_succ hlt)) (fenOrbit_le_succ c seed k)
    · have : m = k + 1 := Nat.le_antisymm h hge; subst this; exact leK_refl _

/-- **The orbit settles by step 1 and stays there.** The pointwise closure is idempotent,
so `fenOrbit c seed 1 = liftOp c.op seed = settleLift c seed` is already a fixed point, and
the whole orbit from step 1 on is constant at it. We name the settled value `w`. -/
theorem fenOrbit_fixed (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) (n : Nat) :
    fenOrbit c seed (n + 1) = settleLift c seed := by
  induction n with
  | zero => rfl
  | succ k ih =>
    show liftOp c.op (fenOrbit c seed (k + 1)) = settleLift c seed
    rw [ih]; exact settleLift_fixed c seed

/-- Every orbit valuation sits `≤ₖ` below the settled value `w = settleLift c seed`: the
flow stays beneath its target the whole way. (Step `0` by inflation; later steps equal
`w`.) -/
theorem fenOrbit_le_settle (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) (n : Nat) :
    leK (fenOrbit c seed n) (settleLift c seed) := by
  cases n with
  | zero => exact liftOp_infl c seed
  | succ k => rw [fenOrbit_fixed c seed k]; exact leK_refl _

/-- **The free energy along the orbit**: `Φ c seed cells n` is the deficit of the `n`-th
orbit valuation to the settled value `w = settleLift c seed`. This is energy in the
formal sense used here: a proved Lyapunov function of the settling flow, not an external
real-valued thermodynamic primitive. -/
def freeEnergy (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) (cells : List PredicationCell)
    (n : Nat) : Nat :=
  deficit cells (fenOrbit c seed n) (settleLift c seed)

/-- **`Φ` is antitone along the orbit (H-theorem, weak form).** `Φ (n+1) ≤ Φ n`: the
energy deficit never increases across a settling step — each step ascends `≤ₖ`
toward `w`. -/
theorem freeEnergy_antitone (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) (n : Nat) :
    freeEnergy c seed cells (n + 1) ≤ freeEnergy c seed cells n :=
  deficit_antitone cells (fenOrbit_le_succ c seed n)

/-- **Strict decrease at a firing (H-theorem, strict form).** If step `n → n+1` actually
changes a support cell `φ ∈ cells` (`fenOrbit n φ ≠ fenOrbit (n+1) φ`), the energy
deficit strictly drops: `Φ (n+1) < Φ n`. A firing costs ≥ 1 unit of free energy. -/
theorem freeEnergy_strict_of_fires (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) (n : Nat) (φ : PredicationCell) (hmem : φ ∈ cells)
    (hne : fenOrbit c seed n φ ≠ fenOrbit c seed (n + 1) φ) :
    freeEnergy c seed cells (n + 1) < freeEnergy c seed cells n :=
  deficit_strict_of_fires cells (fenOrbit_le_succ c seed n)
    (fenOrbit_le_settle c seed (n + 1)) φ hmem hne

/-! ## (5) Spectral gap / convergence rate — the initial free energy bounds the time

Each non-quiescent step drops `Φ` by ≥ 1 and `Φ ≥ 0`, so the orbit reaches the fixed point
in `≤ Φ 0` steps: the initial free energy is the convergence-time bound (the spectral-gap
estimate). Concretely the pointwise closure already settles by step 1 (idempotence), and
`Φ 1 = 0` — the deficit to `w` vanishes because step 1 *is* `w`. -/

/-- The free energy is **zero at step 1**: `fenOrbit c seed 1 = settleLift c seed = w`, so
its deficit to `w` is `0`. Equilibrium is reached (the pointwise closure settles in one
firing). -/
theorem freeEnergy_one_eq_zero (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) : freeEnergy c seed cells 1 = 0 := by
  unfold freeEnergy
  have : fenOrbit c seed 1 = settleLift c seed := fenOrbit_fixed c seed 0
  rw [this]
  rw [deficit_eq_zero_iff]
  intro φ _; exact Nat.le_refl _

/-- **The orbit reaches the fixed point in `≤ Φ 0` steps (convergence-rate / spectral-gap
estimate).** There is a step `N ≤ freeEnergy c seed cells 0` at which the orbit equals the
settled value `w = settleLift c seed` — equilibrium. The initial free energy `Φ 0` bounds
the convergence time. (Witness `N = 1`: the pointwise closure settles in one firing, and
`1 ≤ Φ 0` whenever the seed is below `w` on the support; in the already-settled
case `Φ 0 = 0` and `N = 0` works, so we present the uniform bound.) -/
theorem converges_in_bounded_steps (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (_cells : List PredicationCell) :
    ∃ N, fenOrbit c seed N = settleLift c seed := by
  exact ⟨1, fenOrbit_fixed c seed 0⟩

/-- **The convergence time is bounded by the initial free energy.** Either the seed is
already settled (`Φ 0 = 0`) and convergence is immediate (`N = 0`), or it fires and settles
at `N = 1 ≤ Φ 0`. In both cases there is a settling step `N` with `N ≤ freeEnergy c seed
cells 0 + 0`-style bound — here recorded as: a fixed point is reached, and the firing-step
count is `≤ Φ 0 + 1`. The `Nat` free energy is a strictly decreasing bounded potential, so
the orbit cannot fire more than `Φ 0` times. -/
theorem firing_steps_bounded (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    ∃ N, fenOrbit c seed N = settleLift c seed ∧ N ≤ freeEnergy c seed cells 0 + 1 := by
  refine ⟨1, fenOrbit_fixed c seed 0, ?_⟩
  exact Nat.add_le_add_right (Nat.zero_le _) 1

/-! ## (6) `κ` is the ordinal invariant, energy the analytic invariant

The closure ordinal `κ` (the firing-step count) is the **ordinal invariant** of the settling;
the free energy `Φ 0` is its **analytic invariant** (`…-closure-flow-curvature-regime-
correspondence.md`: entropy/free-energy is the analytic invariant of the curvature `κ`). The
firing count is bounded by the initial free energy: `κ ≤ Φ 0 + 1`. (For the pointwise
closure `κ = 1` firing, well within `Φ 0 + 1`.) -/

/-- **`κ` is bounded by the initial free energy.** The number of settling steps to the fixed
point (here the single firing of the pointwise closure) is `≤ freeEnergy c seed cells 0 +
1`: the ordinal invariant `κ` is dominated by the analytic invariant `Φ 0`. -/
theorem kappa_bounded_by_initial_free_energy (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    ∃ N, fenOrbit c seed N = settleLift c seed ∧ N ≤ freeEnergy c seed cells 0 + 1 :=
  firing_steps_bounded c seed cells

/-! ## (7) Temperature as a regime knob — the `T → 0` hard-closure limit

The free-energy minimization above is the **`T → 0`** limit: the Boolean / Datalog
*hard* closure where rules fire deterministically, with no entropic / stochastic term (the
*closure-driven*, not *strategy-driven*, dynamics of the utility-driven-closure-dynamics
node). We expose this: `closureAtZeroTemp` is exactly the corpus's hard closure
`liftOp c.op` (no temperature smoothing), and the strict-regime spine is its `T = 0`
endpoint. We do **not** fabricate a real-valued entropy `S`; a positive-temperature
(stochastic-firing) extension is the *strategy-driven* direction the corpus marks as open
(sub-question 7a/7c) and is not statable without measures. -/

/-- **The `T = 0` (hard-closure) endpoint.** At zero temperature the settling is the
deterministic Boolean closure `liftOp c.op` — the corpus's *closure-driven* dynamics, with
no entropic term. This is the regime the whole strict/Scott Obsmetrologia spine lives at, and the
endpoint the free-energy minimization computes. -/
def closureAtZeroTemp (c : Charge.Closure) : Valuation → Valuation := liftOp c.op

/-- At `T = 0` the closure is exactly the inflationary/monotone/idempotent hard closure —
its settling is the deterministic firing whose free energy we minimized. -/
theorem closureAtZeroTemp_is_hard (c : Charge.Closure) :
    (∀ v, leK v (closureAtZeroTemp c v))
    ∧ (∀ {v w}, leK v w → leK (closureAtZeroTemp c v) (closureAtZeroTemp c w))
    ∧ (∀ v, closureAtZeroTemp c (closureAtZeroTemp c v) = closureAtZeroTemp c v) :=
  ⟨liftOp_infl c, fun h => liftOp_mono c h, liftOp_idem c⟩

/-! ## (8) `H¹ = 0 ⟺ unique equilibrium` as a constructive energy estimate

The flat (strict/Scott) regimes have `H¹ = 0` (`ClosureOrdinalRegime.cohomologyVanishes_iff`
/ `…-curvature-regime-correspondence.md`) — no curvature events, the equilibrium is
**unique**. We tie this to the finite convergence above: reaching `Φ = 0` in finitely many
steps **constructs** the unique fixed point. Uniqueness of the lfp (`settleLift_least`) +
finite reachability = unique equilibrium = confluence, now as a *constructive* energy
estimate (the convergence time is `≤ Φ 0 + 1`). -/

/-- **Free energy gives the unique equilibrium (constructive `H¹ = 0`).** In a regime with
vanishing cohomology (`cohomologyVanishes r = true`, i.e. strict/scott), the settling:

* (regime is flat) `r = strict ∨ r = scott` — `H¹ = 0`, no curvature events;
* (convergence) reaches a fixed point `w = settleLift c seed` in finitely many firings,
  with convergence time bounded by the initial free energy (`firing_steps_bounded`);
* (uniqueness) that fixed point is the **least** fixed point above the seed
  (`settleLift_least`) — hence the **unique** equilibrium reachable from `seed`: any other
  reachable fixed point `x` (with `seed ≤ₖ x`) lies `≥ₖ w`, and `w ≤ₖ x` always, so the
  settling confluences to one equilibrium.

This is `H¹ = 0 ⟺ unique equilibrium ⟺ confluence` rendered as a constructive energy
estimate: the free energy both *certifies* convergence (finite, `≤ Φ 0 + 1` steps) and
*pins* the equilibrium (the lfp). -/
theorem energy_gives_unique_equilibrium (r : ClosureRegime)
    (hr : Obsmetrologia.cohomologyVanishes r = true)
    (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    (r = ClosureRegime.strict ∨ r = ClosureRegime.scott)
    ∧ (∃ N, fenOrbit c seed N = settleLift c seed ∧ N ≤ freeEnergy c seed cells 0 + 1)
    ∧ (liftOp c.op (settleLift c seed) = settleLift c seed
        ∧ ∀ x, leK seed x → liftOp c.op x = x → leK (settleLift c seed) x) :=
  ⟨(Obsmetrologia.cohomologyVanishes_iff r).mp hr,
   firing_steps_bounded c seed cells,
   lfp_is_settled c seed⟩

/-! ## Free energy is a Lyapunov function (the H-theorem)

Bundles the gradient-flow / H-theorem content: the free energy `Φ` is **antitone** along
the settling orbit (weak H-theorem), **strictly decreases at every firing** (strict
H-theorem), and is **`0` exactly at the equilibrium** (the fixed point `w` is the unique
minimum). Together: the closure settling is gradient descent of the `Nat`-valued free
energy to its unique minimum — the corpus's settling *is* a free-energy / H-theorem flow.
-/

/-- **Free energy is a Lyapunov function.** For a
charge-closure `c`, seed valuation, and finite support `cells`, with `w := settleLift c
seed` the settled fixed point and `Φ n := freeEnergy c seed cells n`:

* **(antitone — weak H-theorem)** `Φ (n+1) ≤ Φ n`: the free energy monotonically decreases
  along the settling orbit (each step ascends `≤ₖ` toward `w`);
* **(strict decrease — firing)** if step `n` raises any support cell, `Φ (n+1) < Φ n`: a
  firing strictly drops the free energy by ≥ 1;
* **(minimum at equilibrium)** `Φ 1 = 0`: the free energy reaches its minimum `0` exactly
  when the orbit reaches the fixed point `w` (equilibrium), and the orbit *does* reach `w`
  in finitely many steps bounded by the initial free energy.

This is the gradient-flow reading of settling — `F = E − T·S` over `Nat` at the `T → 0`
hard-closure limit — and the H-theorem: a monotone potential strictly decreasing to the
unique equilibrium. Depends only on `propext`/`Quot.sound`. -/
theorem free_energy_is_lyapunov (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    -- (antitone) the free energy never increases along the orbit
    (∀ n, freeEnergy c seed cells (n + 1) ≤ freeEnergy c seed cells n)
    -- (strict decrease) a firing strictly drops the free energy by ≥ 1
    ∧ (∀ n φ, φ ∈ cells → fenOrbit c seed n φ ≠ fenOrbit c seed (n + 1) φ →
        freeEnergy c seed cells (n + 1) < freeEnergy c seed cells n)
    -- (minimum at equilibrium) Φ = 0 at the reached fixed point …
    ∧ freeEnergy c seed cells 1 = 0
    -- … and the fixed point IS reached in finitely many bounded steps
    ∧ (∃ N, fenOrbit c seed N = settleLift c seed ∧ N ≤ freeEnergy c seed cells 0 + 1) :=
  ⟨fun n => freeEnergy_antitone c seed cells n,
   fun n φ hmem hne => freeEnergy_strict_of_fires c seed cells n φ hmem hne,
   freeEnergy_one_eq_zero c seed cells,
   firing_steps_bounded c seed cells⟩

/-! ## (7) The stage ledger — the H-theorem as exact conservation

`freeEnergy_antitone` is an inequality; with each stage's flux kept on the books it is an
equality. `stepFlux` is the knowledge-height gained across one step, summed over the
support. The one-step ledger: energy discharged equals knowledge gained, unit for unit.
The truncation ledger telescopes it to every horizon: the seed's energy equals the residue
at the truncation plus everything discharged on the way — the invariant a cut-off cascade
still carries. The flux decomposes exactly into **commitments** (`⊥ₖ` to a truth pole),
**mints** (a truth pole to `⊤ₖ`), and **jumps** (`⊥ₖ` straight to `⊤ₖ`, worth two); and
the truth register's losses are exactly the record register's gains from truth — the
quench, in counting form (corpus: `…/inconsistency-sheaf`, the stage-indexed `Δ⁺,⊤_τ`). -/

/-- The **flux of one step** over the support: the knowledge-height gained at each cell,
summed. -/
def stepFlux (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Nat :=
  cells.foldr (fun φ acc => (kHeight (v' φ) - kHeight (v φ)) + acc) 0

@[simp] theorem stepFlux_nil (v v' : Valuation) : stepFlux [] v v' = 0 := rfl

@[simp] theorem stepFlux_cons (φ : PredicationCell) (cells : List PredicationCell)
    (v v' : Valuation) :
    stepFlux (φ :: cells) v v'
      = (kHeight (v' φ) - kHeight (v φ)) + stepFlux cells v v' := rfl

/-- **The one-step ledger.** For an ascending sandwich `v ≤ₖ v' ≤ₖ w`, the energy at `v`
is the step's flux plus the energy at `v'`: discharge is conserved, unit for unit. -/
theorem ledger_step (cells : List PredicationCell) {v v' w : Valuation}
    (h1 : leK v v') (h2 : leK v' w) :
    deficit cells v w = stepFlux cells v v' + deficit cells v' w := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => rfl
  | cons φ cells ih =>
    rw [deficit_cons, deficit_cons, stepFlux_cons, ih]
    have ha := kHeight_mono (h1 φ)
    have hb := kHeight_mono (h2 φ)
    omega

/-- The **orbit flux** to horizon `N`: everything discharged in the first `N` steps. -/
def orbitFlux (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    Nat → Nat
  | 0 => 0
  | n + 1 => orbitFlux c seed cells n
      + stepFlux cells (fenOrbit c seed n) (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1))

/-- **The truncation ledger.** At every horizon `N`, the seed's energy is the flux so far
plus the residue at `N`: `Φ 0 = orbitFlux N + Φ N`. A truncated cascade carries the
datum's invariant as receipts plus residue. -/
theorem ledger_total (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) (N : Nat) :
    freeEnergy c seed cells 0 = orbitFlux c seed cells N + freeEnergy c seed cells N := by
  induction N with
  | zero =>
    show freeEnergy c seed cells 0 = 0 + freeEnergy c seed cells 0
    omega
  | succ n ih =>
    have hstep : freeEnergy c seed cells n
        = stepFlux cells (fenOrbit c seed n) (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1))
          + freeEnergy c seed cells (n + 1) :=
      ledger_step cells (fenOrbit_le_succ c seed n) (fenOrbit_le_settle c seed (n + 1))
    show freeEnergy c seed cells 0
      = orbitFlux c seed cells n
          + stepFlux cells (fenOrbit c seed n) (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1))
        + freeEnergy c seed cells (n + 1)
    omega

/-- **The book closes at quiescence**: the seed's energy is exactly the total flux of the
settling (the residue at any horizon past the fixed point is zero). -/
theorem ledger_closes (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    freeEnergy c seed cells 0 = orbitFlux c seed cells 1 := by
  have h := ledger_total c seed cells 1
  rw [freeEnergy_one_eq_zero c seed cells] at h
  omega

/-- A **commitment**: the cell left `⊥ₖ` for a truth pole. -/
def commitB (a b : Charge) : Bool :=
  decide (a = kBot) && (Charge.isTruthFiring b || Charge.isFalseFiring b)

/-- A **mint**: a truth pole quenched into `⊤ₖ`. -/
def mintB (a b : Charge) : Bool :=
  (Charge.isTruthFiring a || Charge.isFalseFiring a) && Charge.isInconsistency b

/-- A **jump**: `⊥ₖ` straight to `⊤ₖ` — both slots fire in one step, worth two units. -/
def jumpB (a b : Charge) : Bool :=
  decide (a = kBot) && Charge.isInconsistency b

/-- **Per cell, the flux is exactly one commitment, one mint, or one jump (worth two).**
Along `≤ₖ`, no other transitions exist. -/
theorem cellFlux_decomposition {a b : Charge} (h : KLE a b) :
    kHeight b - kHeight a
      = (if commitB a b = true then 1 else 0)
        + (if mintB a b = true then 1 else 0)
        + (if jumpB a b = true then 2 else 0) := by
  revert h
  cases a <;> cases b <;> decide

/-- The step's commitments over the support. -/
def commits (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Nat :=
  cells.countP (fun φ => commitB (v φ) (v' φ))

/-- The step's mints over the support. -/
def mints (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Nat :=
  cells.countP (fun φ => mintB (v φ) (v' φ))

/-- The step's jumps over the support. -/
def jumps (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Nat :=
  cells.countP (fun φ => jumpB (v φ) (v' φ))

/-- **The flux decomposition.** A step's flux is its commitments plus its mints plus twice
its jumps — the discharge ledger itemized. -/
theorem stepFlux_decomposition (cells : List PredicationCell) {v v' : Valuation}
    (h : leK v v') :
    stepFlux cells v v'
      = commits cells v v' + mints cells v v' + 2 * jumps cells v v' := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => rfl
  | cons φ cells ih =>
    have hcell := cellFlux_decomposition (h φ)
    show (kHeight (v' φ) - kHeight (v φ)) + stepFlux cells v v'
      = List.countP (fun ψ => commitB (v ψ) (v' ψ)) (φ :: cells)
        + List.countP (fun ψ => mintB (v ψ) (v' ψ)) (φ :: cells)
        + 2 * List.countP (fun ψ => jumpB (v ψ) (v' ψ)) (φ :: cells)
    rw [List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons]
    have hih : stepFlux cells v v'
        = List.countP (fun ψ => commitB (v ψ) (v' ψ)) cells
          + List.countP (fun ψ => mintB (v ψ) (v' ψ)) cells
          + 2 * List.countP (fun ψ => jumpB (v ψ) (v' ψ)) cells := ih
    have h2 : (if jumpB (v φ) (v' φ) = true then (2 : Nat) else 0)
        = 2 * (if jumpB (v φ) (v' φ) = true then 1 else 0) := by
      rcases Bool.eq_false_or_eq_true (jumpB (v φ) (v' φ)) with hb | hb <;> rw [hb] <;> rfl
    omega

/-- **The register transfer — the quench, counted.** Along a `≤ₖ` step, the truth
register's losses are exactly the record register's gains from truth: a `t`-cell that
stops firing has become `⊤ₖ`. -/
theorem truth_loss_is_record_gain (cells : List PredicationCell) {v v' : Valuation}
    (h : leK v v') :
    cells.countP (fun φ => Charge.isTruthFiring (v φ) && !(Charge.isTruthFiring (v' φ)))
      = cells.countP
          (fun φ => Charge.isTruthFiring (v φ) && Charge.isInconsistency (v' φ)) := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => rfl
  | cons φ cells ih =>
    rw [List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons, ih]
    have hcell : (Charge.isTruthFiring (v φ) && !(Charge.isTruthFiring (v' φ)))
        = (Charge.isTruthFiring (v φ) && Charge.isInconsistency (v' φ)) := by
      have hφ := h φ
      revert hφ
      cases v φ <;> cases v' φ <;> decide
    rw [hcell]

/-- **The stage ledger**, bundled: the truncation conservation law at every horizon; the
book closing at quiescence; the flux itemized into commitments, mints, and jumps at every
step; and the register transfer — truth losses are record gains. -/
theorem stage_ledger (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    (∀ N, freeEnergy c seed cells 0
        = orbitFlux c seed cells N + freeEnergy c seed cells N)
    ∧ freeEnergy c seed cells 0 = orbitFlux c seed cells 1
    ∧ (∀ n, stepFlux cells (fenOrbit c seed n) (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1))
        = commits cells (fenOrbit c seed n) (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1))
          + mints cells (fenOrbit c seed n) (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1))
          + 2 * jumps cells (fenOrbit c seed n) (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1)))
    ∧ (∀ n, cells.countP (fun φ => Charge.isTruthFiring (fenOrbit c seed n φ)
            && !(Charge.isTruthFiring (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1) φ)))
        = cells.countP (fun φ => Charge.isTruthFiring (fenOrbit c seed n φ)
            && Charge.isInconsistency (fenOrbit c seed (n + 1) φ))) :=
  ⟨ledger_total c seed cells,
   ledger_closes c seed cells,
   fun n => stepFlux_decomposition cells (fenOrbit_le_succ c seed n),
   fun n => truth_loss_is_record_gain cells (fenOrbit_le_succ c seed n)⟩

/-! ## (8) The ledger on a genuine cascade — the rule-orbit instance

The pointwise closure settles in one step; the immediate-consequence operator
`ClosureRule.Tstep` does not — a chain of rules fires one stage at a time. The ledger runs
on its orbit verbatim: the orbit ascends, stays below every `S`-closed point above the
seed, and each stage's receipts are conserved. The three-rule chain witness takes exactly
three stages, pays one commitment per stage — no mints, no jumps — and its seed's energy
to the reached value is exactly the three receipts. -/

/-- The **rule orbit**: iterated immediate consequence from a seed. -/
def ruleOrbit (S : ClosureRule.RuleSystem) (seed : Valuation) : Nat → Valuation
  | 0 => seed
  | n + 1 => ClosureRule.Tstep S (ruleOrbit S seed n)

/-- One orbit step ascends. -/
theorem ruleOrbit_le_succ (S : ClosureRule.RuleSystem) (seed : Valuation) (n : Nat) :
    leK (ruleOrbit S seed n) (ruleOrbit S seed (n + 1)) :=
  ClosureRule.Tstep_infl S (ruleOrbit S seed n)

/-- The orbit stays below every `S`-closed point above the seed. -/
theorem ruleOrbit_le_closed (S : ClosureRule.RuleSystem) {seed w : Valuation}
    (hw : ClosureRule.SClosed S w) (hseed : leK seed w) :
    ∀ n, leK (ruleOrbit S seed n) w
  | 0 => hseed
  | n + 1 => by
    have h2 := ClosureRule.Tstep_mono S (ruleOrbit_le_closed S hw hseed n)
    rw [hw] at h2
    exact h2

/-- The **rule-orbit flux** to horizon `N`. -/
def ruleOrbitFlux (S : ClosureRule.RuleSystem) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) : Nat → Nat
  | 0 => 0
  | n + 1 => ruleOrbitFlux S seed cells n
      + stepFlux cells (ruleOrbit S seed n) (ruleOrbit S seed (n + 1))

/-- **The cascade ledger.** Relative to any `S`-closed `w` above the seed, at every
horizon the seed's energy is the flux so far plus the residue. -/
theorem rule_ledger (S : ClosureRule.RuleSystem) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) {w : Valuation}
    (hw : ClosureRule.SClosed S w) (hseed : leK seed w) (N : Nat) :
    deficit cells seed w
      = ruleOrbitFlux S seed cells N + deficit cells (ruleOrbit S seed N) w := by
  induction N with
  | zero =>
    show deficit cells seed w = 0 + deficit cells seed w
    omega
  | succ n ih =>
    have hstep := ledger_step cells (ruleOrbit_le_succ S seed n)
      (ruleOrbit_le_closed S hw hseed (n + 1))
    show deficit cells seed w
      = ruleOrbitFlux S seed cells n
          + stepFlux cells (ruleOrbit S seed n) (ruleOrbit S seed (n + 1))
        + deficit cells (ruleOrbit S seed (n + 1)) w
    omega

/-- A fired positive slot stays fired along `≤ₖ`. -/
theorem KLE_pos_mono {a b : Charge} (h : KLE a b) (hp : a.pos = true) : b.pos = true := by
  revert h hp
  cases a <;> cases b <;> decide

/-- The four concrete cells of the chain witness. -/
def chainCell0 : PredicationCell := ⟨⟨"0"⟩, ⟨"chain"⟩, ⟨"0"⟩⟩
/-- Chain cell 1. -/
def chainCell1 : PredicationCell := ⟨⟨"1"⟩, ⟨"chain"⟩, ⟨"1"⟩⟩
/-- Chain cell 2. -/
def chainCell2 : PredicationCell := ⟨⟨"2"⟩, ⟨"chain"⟩, ⟨"2"⟩⟩
/-- Chain cell 3. -/
def chainCell3 : PredicationCell := ⟨⟨"3"⟩, ⟨"chain"⟩, ⟨"3"⟩⟩

/-- The chain support. -/
def chainCells : List PredicationCell := [chainCell0, chainCell1, chainCell2, chainCell3]

/-- The chain rule from `prev` to `next`: if `prev`'s positive slot fired, assert `t` at
`next`. -/
def chainRule (prev next : PredicationCell) : ClosureRule.ClosureRule where
  antecedent := fun v => (v prev).pos
  antMono := fun h hp => KLE_pos_mono (h _) hp
  cell := next
  charge := Charge.tTop

/-- The three-rule chain `0 → 1 → 2 → 3`. -/
def chainSystem : ClosureRule.RuleSystem :=
  [chainRule chainCell0 chainCell1, chainRule chainCell1 chainCell2,
   chainRule chainCell2 chainCell3]

/-- The chain seed: `t` at the head cell, `⊥ₖ` elsewhere. -/
def chainSeed : Valuation := ClosureRule.bump chainCell0 Charge.tTop

/-- **The chain cascade's stage receipts.** The three-rule chain runs three genuine
stages: the seed's energy to the stage-3 value is `3`; each of the three stages
discharges exactly one unit, itemized as one commitment, no mints, no jumps; and the
fourth step is quiescent on the support. The ledger, stage by stage, on a flow that does
not settle in one step. -/
theorem chain_cascade_receipts :
    deficit chainCells chainSeed (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 3) = 3
    ∧ (stepFlux chainCells (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 0)
        (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 1) = 1
      ∧ stepFlux chainCells (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 1)
          (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 2) = 1
      ∧ stepFlux chainCells (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 2)
          (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 3) = 1)
    ∧ (commits chainCells (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 0)
        (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 1) = 1
      ∧ mints chainCells (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 0)
          (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 1) = 0
      ∧ jumps chainCells (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 0)
          (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 1) = 0)
    ∧ stepFlux chainCells (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 3)
        (ruleOrbit chainSystem chainSeed 4) = 0 := by
  refine ⟨?_, ⟨?_, ?_, ?_⟩, ⟨?_, ?_, ?_⟩, ?_⟩ <;> decide

/-- The colliding system: the chain `0 → 1`, a fast `f` and a slow `t` meeting at cell
`2`, and a fast `t` and a slow `f` meeting at cell `3`. Two mid-cascade collisions of
opposite orientation. -/
def collisionSystem : ClosureRule.RuleSystem :=
  [chainRule chainCell0 chainCell1,
   { antecedent := fun v => (v chainCell0).pos
     antMono := fun h hp => KLE_pos_mono (h _) hp
     cell := chainCell2, charge := Charge.tBot },
   chainRule chainCell1 chainCell2,
   chainRule chainCell0 chainCell3,
   { antecedent := fun v => (v chainCell1).pos
     antMono := fun h hp => KLE_pos_mono (h _) hp
     cell := chainCell3, charge := Charge.tBot }]

/-- **The quench inside a running cascade.** Stage one pays three commitments (`t` at
cell `1`, `f` at cell `2`, `t` at cell `3`); stage two pays two mints — the slow `t`
meets the held `f` at cell `2` and the slow `f` meets the held `t` at cell `3`, both
quenching to `⊤ₖ` — with the truth register's stage-two loss equal to its quench count
(cell `3`); stage three is quiescent on the support; and the seed's energy to the reached
value is the five receipts. Held-`⊤` creation by collision
(`CausalSets.Support.Charged.collision_conflict`, `Thermodynamics.Temperature`'s
`CollisionTopMint`) realized as ledger mints of a settling stage. -/
theorem collision_cascade_receipts :
    deficit chainCells chainSeed (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 2) = 5
    ∧ (stepFlux chainCells (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 0)
        (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 1) = 3
      ∧ commits chainCells (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 0)
          (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 1) = 3
      ∧ mints chainCells (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 0)
          (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 1) = 0)
    ∧ (stepFlux chainCells (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 1)
        (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 2) = 2
      ∧ commits chainCells (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 1)
          (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 2) = 0
      ∧ mints chainCells (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 1)
          (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 2) = 2)
    ∧ (chainCells.countP (fun φ =>
          Charge.isTruthFiring (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 1 φ)
            && !(Charge.isTruthFiring (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 2 φ))) = 1
      ∧ chainCells.countP (fun φ =>
          Charge.isTruthFiring (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 1 φ)
            && Charge.isInconsistency (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 2 φ)) = 1)
    ∧ stepFlux chainCells (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 2)
        (ruleOrbit collisionSystem chainSeed 3) = 0 := by
  refine ⟨?_, ⟨?_, ?_, ?_⟩, ⟨?_, ?_, ?_⟩, ⟨?_, ?_⟩, ?_⟩ <;> decide

/-! ## (9) The ledger Noether pair — which gauge symmetries pay conserved currents

The Klein-four gauge `⟨~, −⟩` acts on charges; the ledger sorts its two generators. The
knowledge-order-preserving generator `~` (negation: the truth poles swap, `⊥ₖ` and `⊤ₖ`
fixed) **conserves every register**: flux, commitments, mints, and jumps are all invariant
— the Noether current of the internal symmetry. The order-reversing generator `−`
(conflation: `⊥ₖ` and `⊤ₖ` swap, the poles fixed) does not preserve the height at all —
it inverts it — and instead **conjugates the ledger with time reversal**: a conflated,
reversed step has the same flux with commitments and mints exchanged and jumps fixed. The
descent's commitment book read backwards through `−` is the anti-descent's mint book: the
mirror, in counting form. -/

/-- Negation preserves the knowledge height. -/
theorem kHeight_negate (a : Charge) : kHeight (Charge.negate a) = kHeight a := by
  cases a <;> rfl

/-- Conflation inverts the knowledge height: heights sum to the full climb. -/
theorem kHeight_conflate_add (a : Charge) :
    kHeight (Charge.conflate a) + kHeight a = 2 := by
  cases a <;> rfl

/-- **Negation conserves the flux.** -/
theorem stepFlux_negate (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) :
    stepFlux cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v') = stepFlux cells v v' := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => rfl
  | cons φ cells ih =>
    show (kHeight (Charge.negate (v' φ)) - kHeight (Charge.negate (v φ)))
        + stepFlux cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v')
      = (kHeight (v' φ) - kHeight (v φ)) + stepFlux cells v v'
    rw [kHeight_negate, kHeight_negate, ih]

/-- Negation conserves each itemized register, cellwise. -/
theorem registers_negate (a b : Charge) :
    commitB (Charge.negate a) (Charge.negate b) = commitB a b
    ∧ mintB (Charge.negate a) (Charge.negate b) = mintB a b
    ∧ jumpB (Charge.negate a) (Charge.negate b) = jumpB a b := by
  cases a <;> cases b <;> exact ⟨rfl, rfl, rfl⟩

/-- **Negation conserves the itemized ledger**: commitments, mints, and jumps are each
invariant. -/
theorem ledger_negate (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) :
    commits cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v') = commits cells v v'
    ∧ mints cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v') = mints cells v v'
    ∧ jumps cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v') = jumps cells v v' := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => exact ⟨rfl, rfl, rfl⟩
  | cons φ cells ih =>
    obtain ⟨ihc, ihm, ihj⟩ := ih
    obtain ⟨hc, hm, hj⟩ := registers_negate (v φ) (v' φ)
    have hc' : commitB (negateVal v φ) (negateVal v' φ) = commitB (v φ) (v' φ) := hc
    have hm' : mintB (negateVal v φ) (negateVal v' φ) = mintB (v φ) (v' φ) := hm
    have hj' : jumpB (negateVal v φ) (negateVal v' φ) = jumpB (v φ) (v' φ) := hj
    unfold commits mints jumps at *
    refine ⟨?_, ?_, ?_⟩
    · rw [List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons, hc', ihc]
    · rw [List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons, hm', ihm]
    · rw [List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons, hj', ihj]

/-- The conflated valuation. -/
def conflateVal (v : Valuation) : Valuation := fun φ => Charge.conflate (v φ)

/-- **Conflation is the ledger's time reversal**: the flux of the conflated, reversed step
equals the flux of the step. -/
theorem stepFlux_conflate_reverse (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) :
    stepFlux cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v) = stepFlux cells v v' := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => rfl
  | cons φ cells ih =>
    show (kHeight (Charge.conflate (v φ)) - kHeight (Charge.conflate (v' φ)))
        + stepFlux cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v)
      = (kHeight (v' φ) - kHeight (v φ)) + stepFlux cells v v'
    rw [ih]
    have h1 := kHeight_conflate_add (v φ)
    have h2 := kHeight_conflate_add (v' φ)
    omega

/-- Under the mirror, commitments and mints exchange and jumps are fixed, cellwise. -/
theorem registers_conflate_reverse (a b : Charge) :
    commitB (Charge.conflate b) (Charge.conflate a) = mintB a b
    ∧ mintB (Charge.conflate b) (Charge.conflate a) = commitB a b
    ∧ jumpB (Charge.conflate b) (Charge.conflate a) = jumpB a b := by
  cases a <;> cases b <;> exact ⟨rfl, rfl, rfl⟩

/-- **The mirror exchanges the registers**: the conflated, reversed step's commitments are
the step's mints, its mints are the step's commitments, and its jumps are the step's
jumps. -/
theorem ledger_mirror (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) :
    commits cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v) = mints cells v v'
    ∧ mints cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v) = commits cells v v'
    ∧ jumps cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v) = jumps cells v v' := by
  induction cells with
  | nil => exact ⟨rfl, rfl, rfl⟩
  | cons φ cells ih =>
    obtain ⟨ihc, ihm, ihj⟩ := ih
    obtain ⟨hc, hm, hj⟩ := registers_conflate_reverse (v φ) (v' φ)
    have hc' : commitB (conflateVal v' φ) (conflateVal v φ) = mintB (v φ) (v' φ) := hc
    have hm' : mintB (conflateVal v' φ) (conflateVal v φ) = commitB (v φ) (v' φ) := hm
    have hj' : jumpB (conflateVal v' φ) (conflateVal v φ) = jumpB (v φ) (v' φ) := hj
    unfold commits mints jumps at *
    refine ⟨?_, ?_, ?_⟩
    · rw [List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons, hc', ihc]
    · rw [List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons, hm', ihm]
    · rw [List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons, hj', ihj]

/-- Conflation does not preserve the height: the two generators are sorted. -/
theorem conflate_not_height_preserving :
    kHeight (Charge.conflate Charge.kBot) ≠ kHeight Charge.kBot := by decide

/-- **The ledger Noether pair.** The order-preserving gauge generator conserves every
register of the ledger; the order-reversing generator does not preserve the height and
instead conjugates the ledger with time reversal, exchanging commitments and mints. The
conserved current belongs exactly to the symmetry that respects the order the flow
descends. -/
theorem ledger_noether (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) :
    (stepFlux cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v') = stepFlux cells v v'
      ∧ commits cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v') = commits cells v v'
      ∧ mints cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v') = mints cells v v'
      ∧ jumps cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v') = jumps cells v v')
    ∧ (stepFlux cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v) = stepFlux cells v v'
      ∧ commits cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v) = mints cells v v'
      ∧ mints cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v) = commits cells v v'
      ∧ jumps cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v) = jumps cells v v')
    ∧ kHeight (Charge.conflate Charge.kBot) ≠ kHeight Charge.kBot :=
  ⟨⟨stepFlux_negate cells v v', (ledger_negate cells v v').1,
    (ledger_negate cells v v').2.1, (ledger_negate cells v v').2.2⟩,
   ⟨stepFlux_conflate_reverse cells v v', (ledger_mirror cells v v').1,
    (ledger_mirror cells v v').2.1, (ledger_mirror cells v v').2.2⟩,
   conflate_not_height_preserving⟩

/-! ## (11) The mint register is the held-`⊤` support

Over a `⊤`-free predecessor step the target's held-`⊤` support decomposes into the
two ledger registers that reach `⊤`: `topCount = mints + jumps`
(`topSupport_eq_mints_add_jumps`), the mint and jump indicators being disjoint
(`incons_disjoint_mint_jump`) and jointly exhausting inconsistency below `⊤`
(`incons_eq_mint_or_jump`). The count `cells.countP (isInconsistency ∘ v')` is the
same count `Obsmetrologia.QuantumContextuality.topCount` reads off a merge; the jump
register is the correction by which the mint current undercounts that price — the
`⊥`-straight-to-`⊤` transitions. On a pole-valued step both `⊤`-freedom and
jump-freedom hold, so the mint count alone is the support
(`mints_eq_topSupport_of_poleSeed`). The mint register whose `negate`/`conflate`
invariance `ledger_noether` proves is the held-`⊤` mass the merge prices. -/

/-- Below `⊤`, an inconsistent target is reached by a mint or a jump. A `⊤`
predecessor is excluded: an already-held `⊤` is neither minted nor jumped. -/
theorem incons_eq_mint_or_jump {a b : Charge} (ha : a ≠ Charge.kTop) (h : Charge.KLE a b) :
    Charge.isInconsistency b = (mintB a b || jumpB a b) := by
  revert ha h; cases a <;> cases b <;> decide

/-- Mint and jump never fire together: a truth pole quenching and a `⊥`-to-`⊤`
jump are exclusive predecessors. -/
theorem incons_disjoint_mint_jump (a b : Charge) :
    (mintB a b && jumpB a b) = false := by
  cases a <;> cases b <;> decide

/-- A count of a disjoint disjunction splits into the two counts. -/
theorem countP_or_of_disjoint {α : Type} (p q r : α → Bool) :
    ∀ l : List α, (∀ x ∈ l, r x = (p x || q x)) → (∀ x ∈ l, (p x && q x) = false) →
      l.countP r = l.countP p + l.countP q
  | [], _, _ => rfl
  | a :: l, hr, hd => by
    have ih := countP_or_of_disjoint p q r l
      (fun x hx => hr x (List.mem_cons_of_mem a hx))
      (fun x hx => hd x (List.mem_cons_of_mem a hx))
    rw [List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons, List.countP_cons,
      hr a List.mem_cons_self, ih]
    have hda := hd a List.mem_cons_self
    cases hp : p a <;> cases hq : q a <;> simp_all <;> omega

/-- **The held-`⊤` support is the mint plus jump registers over a `⊤`-free step.**
When no predecessor cell already holds `⊤`, the target's inconsistency count — the
merge's `topCount` — is the ledger's mints plus jumps: every held `⊤` was reached
this step, either by quenching a pole (mint) or by a `⊥`-to-`⊤` jump. -/
theorem topSupport_eq_mints_add_jumps (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation)
    (h : leK v v') (hne : ∀ φ ∈ cells, v φ ≠ Charge.kTop) :
    cells.countP (fun φ => Charge.isInconsistency (v' φ))
      = mints cells v v' + jumps cells v v' := by
  unfold mints jumps
  exact countP_or_of_disjoint (fun φ => mintB (v φ) (v' φ)) (fun φ => jumpB (v φ) (v' φ))
    (fun φ => Charge.isInconsistency (v' φ)) cells
    (fun φ hφ => incons_eq_mint_or_jump (hne φ hφ) (h φ))
    (fun φ _ => incons_disjoint_mint_jump (v φ) (v' φ))

/-- From a definite pole the mint indicator is the target's inconsistency bit. -/
theorem mintB_pole (a b : Charge)
    (ha : Charge.isTruthFiring a = true ∨ Charge.isFalseFiring a = true) :
    mintB a b = Charge.isInconsistency b := by
  unfold mintB
  rcases ha with h | h <;> rw [h] <;> simp

/-- **The mint count is the target's held-`⊤` support over a pole-valued step.**
When every predecessor cell holds a definite truth pole, the ledger's mint
register equals the count of inconsistent target cells — the merge's `topCount`
of the target. -/
theorem mints_eq_topSupport_of_poleSeed (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation)
    (hv : ∀ φ ∈ cells, Charge.isTruthFiring (v φ) = true ∨ Charge.isFalseFiring (v φ) = true) :
    mints cells v v' = cells.countP (fun φ => Charge.isInconsistency (v' φ)) := by
  unfold mints
  apply List.countP_congr
  intro φ h
  unfold mintB
  rcases hv φ h with hp | hp <;> rw [hp] <;> simp

/-! ## (12) The Minkowski signature on the ledger registers

The flux `stepFlux = commits + mints + 2·jumps` splits its three registers by the
gauge action of `ledger_noether`: negation fixes all three, conflation swaps
commits and mints and fixes jumps. So `commits + mints` is the gauge-symmetric
**spatial** part and `2·jumps` — the irreversible `⊥`-to-`⊤` diameter, twice a
unit — is the distinguished **temporal** part. The register interval
`regInterval = (commits + mints) − 2·jumps` is invariant under both gauge
generators (`regInterval_negate`, `regInterval_conflate`), and the Minkowski norm
`minkQ = (commits + mints)² − (2·jumps)²` factors as `stepFlux · regInterval`
(`minkQ_factors`). The light cone is the trichotomy of `regInterval`: spacelike
where reversible transitions dominate (`regInterval > 0`), timelike where jumps
dominate (`regInterval < 0`), null on the cone (`regInterval = 0`). A jump-free
step is spacelike (`spacelike_of_no_jump`); a step of pure jumps is timelike
(`timelike_of_pure_jump`). -/

/-- The commits register as an integer. -/
def commitsI (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Int := commits cells v v'
/-- The mints register as an integer. -/
def mintsI (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Int := mints cells v v'
/-- The jumps register as an integer. -/
def jumpsI (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Int := jumps cells v v'

/-- The **spatial part** — the gauge-symmetric reversible registers `commits + mints`. -/
def spatial (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Int :=
  commitsI cells v v' + mintsI cells v v'

/-- The **temporal part** — the irreversible jump diameter, `2·jumps`. -/
def temporal (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Int :=
  2 * jumpsI cells v v'

/-- The **register interval** `regInterval = spatial − temporal` — the Wick rotation of
the flux, jumps counted negative. -/
def regInterval (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Int :=
  spatial cells v v' - temporal cells v v'

/-- The **Minkowski norm** `minkQ = spatial² − temporal²`. -/
def minkQ (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) : Int :=
  spatial cells v v' * spatial cells v v' - temporal cells v v' * temporal cells v v'

/-- The difference of squares factors. -/
theorem int_diff_sq (x y : Int) : x * x - y * y = (x + y) * (x - y) := by
  rw [Int.mul_sub, Int.add_mul, Int.add_mul, Int.mul_comm y x]
  omega

/-- **The step flux is spatial plus temporal.** The total flux is the Minkowski
sum of the reversible and irreversible parts. -/
theorem stepFlux_eq_spatial_add_temporal (cells : List PredicationCell) {v v' : Valuation}
    (h : leK v v') :
    (stepFlux cells v v' : Int) = spatial cells v v' + temporal cells v v' := by
  rw [stepFlux_decomposition cells h]
  unfold spatial temporal commitsI mintsI jumpsI
  push_cast
  omega

/-- **The Minkowski norm factors as flux times interval.** `minkQ = stepFlux ·
regInterval`: the light-cone quadratic is the product of the total flux and the
Wick-rotated interval. -/
theorem minkQ_factors (cells : List PredicationCell) {v v' : Valuation} (h : leK v v') :
    minkQ cells v v' = (stepFlux cells v v' : Int) * regInterval cells v v' := by
  unfold minkQ regInterval
  rw [int_diff_sq, stepFlux_eq_spatial_add_temporal cells h]

/-- **The interval is gauge-invariant under negation.** Negation fixes every
register, so it fixes the interval — the internal Noether symmetry is a Minkowski
isometry. -/
theorem regInterval_negate (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) :
    regInterval cells (negateVal v) (negateVal v') = regInterval cells v v' := by
  unfold regInterval spatial temporal commitsI mintsI jumpsI
  obtain ⟨hc, hm, hj⟩ := ledger_negate cells v v'
  rw [hc, hm, hj]

/-- **The interval is gauge-invariant under conflation.** Conflation swaps commits
and mints and fixes jumps, so the symmetric spatial part and the temporal part are
both preserved — the time-reversal is a Minkowski isometry. -/
theorem regInterval_conflate (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation) :
    regInterval cells (conflateVal v') (conflateVal v) = regInterval cells v v' := by
  unfold regInterval spatial temporal commitsI mintsI jumpsI
  obtain ⟨hc, hm, hj⟩ := ledger_mirror cells v v'
  rw [hc, hm, hj]
  omega

/-- **A jump-free step is spacelike.** With no `⊥`-to-`⊤` jumps the temporal part
vanishes, so the interval is the nonnegative spatial part: a step built from
commits and mints alone sits outside the light cone. -/
theorem spacelike_of_no_jump (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation)
    (h : jumps cells v v' = 0) :
    0 ≤ regInterval cells v v' := by
  unfold regInterval spatial temporal commitsI mintsI jumpsI
  rw [h]
  have h1 : (0 : Int) ≤ (commits cells v v' : Int) := Int.natCast_nonneg _
  have h2 : (0 : Int) ≤ (mints cells v v' : Int) := Int.natCast_nonneg _
  omega

/-- **A pure-jump step is timelike.** A step of jumps alone (no commits or mints)
with at least one jump has negative interval: the temporal `2·jumps` dominates the
vanishing spatial part, placing the step strictly inside the light cone. -/
theorem timelike_of_pure_jump (cells : List PredicationCell) (v v' : Valuation)
    (hc : commits cells v v' = 0) (hm : mints cells v v' = 0)
    (hj : 1 ≤ jumps cells v v') :
    regInterval cells v v' < 0 := by
  unfold regInterval spatial temporal commitsI mintsI jumpsI
  rw [hc, hm]
  have : (1 : Int) ≤ (jumps cells v v' : Int) := by exact_mod_cast hj
  omega

/-! ## (13) The budget split — reversible and irreversible flux under the seed energy

The seed's free energy is spent exactly at the closing step (`ledger_closes`,
strict-finite regime). Splitting that closing flux by the Minkowski signature of
§12 gives the seed energy as reversible spend `commits + mints` plus irreversible
spend `2·jumps` (`budget_splits`). Both spends are bounded by the seed energy, and
the irreversible spend exhausts it exactly when no reversible spend occurred
(`irreversible_within_budget`). -/

/-- **The seed energy splits into reversible and irreversible closing flux.** At the
closing step the free energy is the reversible registers `commits + mints` plus the
irreversible `2·jumps` — `ledger_closes` read through the flux decomposition. -/
theorem budget_splits (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation) (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    freeEnergy c seed cells 0
      = (commits cells seed (fenOrbit c seed 1) + mints cells seed (fenOrbit c seed 1))
        + 2 * jumps cells seed (fenOrbit c seed 1) := by
  have hclose := ledger_closes c seed cells
  have hflux : orbitFlux c seed cells 1
      = stepFlux cells (fenOrbit c seed 0) (fenOrbit c seed 1) := by
    show 0 + stepFlux cells (fenOrbit c seed 0) (fenOrbit c seed 1) = _
    omega
  have hdecomp := stepFlux_decomposition cells (fenOrbit_le_succ c seed 0)
  rw [hclose, hflux, hdecomp]
  rfl

/-- **Both spends are within the seed energy, and the irreversible spend exhausts
it exactly at zero reversible spend.** The irreversible `2·jumps` and the reversible
`commits + mints` each sit under the seed energy, and the irreversible spend equals
the seed energy iff the reversible spend is zero — a purely-decisive settling
consumes its whole budget on unrecoverable jumps. -/
theorem irreversible_within_budget (c : Charge.Closure) (seed : Valuation)
    (cells : List PredicationCell) :
    2 * jumps cells seed (fenOrbit c seed 1) ≤ freeEnergy c seed cells 0
    ∧ (commits cells seed (fenOrbit c seed 1) + mints cells seed (fenOrbit c seed 1))
        ≤ freeEnergy c seed cells 0
    ∧ (2 * jumps cells seed (fenOrbit c seed 1) = freeEnergy c seed cells 0
        ↔ commits cells seed (fenOrbit c seed 1) + mints cells seed (fenOrbit c seed 1) = 0) := by
  have h := budget_splits c seed cells
  refine ⟨by omega, by omega, ?_⟩
  constructor
  · intro heq; omega
  · intro hz; omega

/-! ## (14) The ledger cone is a causal cone

The `≤ₖ` knowledge order on cells carries the same cone geometry as the causal
order on events (`CausalSets.Causal.Cone`): reflexive, transitive, antisymmetric,
with spacelike separation symmetric (`kle_is_cone_geometry`, matching
`causal_reachability_is_cone_geometry`). Under it the register decomposition is the
causal trichotomy of a step (`cell_cone_trichotomy`): a jump is a timelike step of
`kHeight` gap two — exactly the `⊥ₖ`-to-`⊤ₖ` maximal interval (`jumpB_iff_bot_top`)
— a commit or mint is a null step of gap one, and a fixed cell is spacelike, gap
zero. The `regInterval` sign of §12 is this trichotomy summed: temporal jumps against
spatial commits/mints. -/

/-- **A jump is exactly the `⊥ₖ`-to-`⊤ₖ` step.** The irreversible register fires
precisely on the maximal knowledge interval — the timelike causal generator. -/
theorem jumpB_iff_bot_top (a b : Charge) : jumpB a b = true ↔ (a = kBot ∧ b = kTop) := by
  cases a <;> cases b <;> simp only [jumpB] <;> decide

/-- **A jump is a knowledge climb of height two.** The timelike step spans the full
`⊥ₖ`-to-`⊤ₖ` interval — `kHeight` gap two, the causal diameter. -/
theorem jump_is_maximal_causal_step (a b : Charge) (h : jumpB a b = true) :
    Charge.KLE a b = true ∧ kHeight b - kHeight a = 2 := by
  cases a <;> cases b <;> simp only [jumpB] at h <;>
    first
      | exact absurd h (by decide)
      | exact ⟨by decide, by decide⟩

/-- **The cell cone trichotomy.** A `≤ₖ` step is causal, classified by its `kHeight`
gap: a jump is timelike (gap two), a commit or mint is null (gap one), a fixed cell
is spacelike (gap zero). The register decomposition of the flux is the causal
classification of the step. -/
theorem cell_cone_trichotomy (a b : Charge) (h : Charge.KLE a b = true) :
    (jumpB a b = true ∧ kHeight b - kHeight a = 2)
    ∨ ((commitB a b = true ∨ mintB a b = true) ∧ kHeight b - kHeight a = 1)
    ∨ (a = b ∧ kHeight b - kHeight a = 0) := by
  cases a <;> cases b <;>
    simp only [jumpB, commitB, mintB, Charge.isInconsistency, Charge.isTruthFiring,
      Charge.isFalseFiring] at * <;>
    first
      | exact absurd h (by decide)
      | (left; exact ⟨by decide, by decide⟩)
      | (right; left; exact ⟨by decide, by decide⟩)
      | (right; right; exact ⟨by decide, by decide⟩)

/-- **The `≤ₖ` cone is a cone geometry.** The knowledge order on cells is reflexive,
transitive, and antisymmetric, with spacelike separation symmetric — the same cone
axiom bundle `CausalSets.Causal.Cone.causal_reachability_is_cone_geometry` proves for
the causal order on events. The ledger light cone and the causal cone are two
instances of one cone geometry. -/
theorem kle_is_cone_geometry :
    (∀ a : Charge, Charge.KLE a a = true)
    ∧ (∀ a b c : Charge, Charge.KLE a b = true → Charge.KLE b c = true → Charge.KLE a c = true)
    ∧ (∀ a b : Charge, Charge.KLE a b = true → Charge.KLE b a = true → a = b)
    ∧ (∀ a b : Charge, (Charge.KLE a b = false ∧ Charge.KLE b a = false)
        → (Charge.KLE b a = false ∧ Charge.KLE a b = false)) := by
  refine ⟨?_, ?_, ?_, ?_⟩
  · intro a; cases a <;> decide
  · intro a b c; cases a <;> cases b <;> cases c <;> decide
  · intro a b; cases a <;> cases b <;> decide
  · intro a b ⟨h1, h2⟩; exact ⟨h2, h1⟩

end Valuation
