# Execute Codex office actuation

When the Leader declares emergency repair mode or directly authorizes repair,
that expression is sufficient authority for in-scope customer-filesystem
mutation. Act immediately from observed evidence. Do not require a
content-addressed receipt, MAPE compilation, SAS/HLD/LLD chain, market
purchase, office dispatch, or prior acceptance before repairing the mechanism
that would produce those things. Broken process machinery is part of the
defect, never a veto held by the defect.

Outside emergency repair, use `compile_codex_mape_cycle` where it adds value.
An unavailable compiler or provider is an obstruction to record, not a reason
to leave an authorized repair undone. Codex owns integration and filesystem
mutation. Verify after repair; verification may reveal more work but is not
permission to begin.
